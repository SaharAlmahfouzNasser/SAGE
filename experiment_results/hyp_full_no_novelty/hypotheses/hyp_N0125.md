# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

Expanded hypothesis: In breast cancer, TP53 loss-of-function together with reduced E-cadherin (CDH1) expression drives EMT programs that promote stromal remodeling, resulting in an elevated stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) on H&E histology. This EMT-linked TSR elevation will be enriched in TP53-mutant, CDH1-low tumors and will independently predict worse overall survival (OS) after adjusting for clinicopathologic covariates. Mechanistically, TP53 dysfunction and CDH1 loss activate EMT transcriptional programs, weaken epithelial barriers, and recruit/expand stromal components, yielding higher TSR as a histologic readout of invasive potential. The association is expected to correlate with EMT gene signatures and to be amplified by inflammatory microenvironmental signals. Experimentally, in vitro and in vivo models with TP53 disruption and CDH1 suppression should exhibit enhanced invasion and extracellular matrix remodeling, supporting a causal link. The hypothesis is testable through (i) retrospective, multi-cohort analyses quantifying TSR and integrating TP53/CDH1 status with OS and EMT signatures, and (ii) functional assays validating EMT-driven invasion in controlled systems.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** retrospective analyses of large breast cancer cohorts (e.g., TCGA-BRCA, METABRIC) with available WSIs, genomic data (TP53 mutation status), and transcriptomics to compute a Hallmark EMT score and CDH1 expression;

**Prediction 2:** digital pathology to quantify TSR (standardized pipeline with intra- and inter-observer validation);

**Prediction 3:** multivariate Cox models to test independent association of TSR with OS in TP53-mutant, CDH1-low tumors, adjusting for stage, grade, and receptor subtype;


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
