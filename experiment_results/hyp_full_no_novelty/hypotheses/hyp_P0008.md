# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

From the Ontologist's synthesis, TMB and local immune contexture (CD4/CD8 TILs) are each linked to outcome in MIBC, yet their prognostic/predictive value is inconsistent and implementation is constrained by accessibility; the added observation of tumor-derived SDC2/TFPI2 methylation in stool suggests an epigenetic/ECM phenotype that could modulate immune-tumor interactions. 2) Tier 1 paths used: (a) TMB — chosen because it mechanistically modifies neoantigen load and is the best molecular determinant of ICI sensitivity; (b) SDC2/TFPI2 methylation — chosen as a tractable, non-invasive epigenetic marker that signals an altered ECM/tumor-shedding phenotype potentially linked to immune exclusion; (c) tissue TILs (operationalized here as image-derived TIL density) — chosen because it is the direct morphologic readout of local adaptive immunity. I built the hypothesis around how TMB and SDC2-related methylation modify the functional impact of image-measured TIL density. 3) Tier 2 support: AI-enabled biomarker accessibility and MSI strengthen the translational path (making combined TIL/TMB/methylation testing feasible); HDL-C and cross-cancer biomarkers (PSA) provide orthogonal metabolic and implementation context but not mechanistic primacy. 4) Why this endpoint: I selected therapy_response (immune checkpoint blockade response proxy) because TMB and TIL density are mechanistically tied to anti-tumor immune recognition and hence to ICI efficacy — this yields the most testable, clinically actionable question. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded using PSA, pZVR, CSF-AD links and other cross-disease signals as core anchors because they lack direct mechanistic connection to MIBC immune biology; TFPI2, CD4, CD8, MSI, HDL-C and AI-access methods are retained as secondary_modifiers for follow-up stratified analyses rather than primary anchor variables to preserve focus?

## Working Hypothesis

In muscle-invasive bladder cancer, the predictive value of high tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) density for immune checkpoint inhibitor (ICI) response is conditional on tumor mutational burden (TMB); high TMB amplifies TIL-mediated anti-tumor activity, whereas SDC2 promoter methylation marks an ECM-remodeling/immune-exclusion phenotype that impairs TIL effector function. Consequently, a composite biomarker integrating tissue TIL density, tissue TMB, and SDC2 methylation status (and optionally stool SDC2/TFPI2 methylation as a non-invasive readout) will better stratify ICI responders. Specifically, the subset with TIL-high, TMB-high, and SDC2 unmethylated tumors will exhibit the highest likelihood of objective response, while TIL-high with TMB-low or SDC2-methylated status will show attenuated or absent benefit despite abundant TILs.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Retrospective cohort analyses with quantified TIL density by digital pathology, tumor TMB (via whole-exome or targeted panels), and tumor SDC2 promoter methylation;

**Prediction 2:** stool assays for SDC2/TFPI2 methylation as non-invasive proxies;

**Prediction 3:** statistical modeling to test interaction terms: TIL density × TMB, and both × SDC2 methylation, plus development of a composite score;


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
