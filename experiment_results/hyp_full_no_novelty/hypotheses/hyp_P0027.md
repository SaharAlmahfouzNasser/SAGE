# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In breast cancer (TCGA-BRCA), high Tumor-Infiltrating Lymphocyte (TIL) density predicts greater response to anti-PD-1/PD-L1 therapy predominantly in tumors with proficient mismatch repair (MMR) via MSH6 and MLH1, which yields a high burden of clonal neoantigens. Conversely, in tumors with MMR dysfunction (loss-of-function in MSH6 and/or MLH1), despite high TIL density, responses to PD-1/PD-L1 blockade are attenuated due to increased neoantigen heterogeneity and subclonal dominance that reduces effective T cell recognition and antigenic clonality. Therefore, the combination of TIL density and MMR status (MSH6/MLH1) will stratify responders more accurately than TIL density alone. TMB will serve as a supporting biomarker, with clonal versus subclonal neoantigen burden mediating the effect. Secondary modifiers, including HLA-DQB1 genotype (antigen presentation efficiency), TP53 mutation status (tumor immunogenicity and genomic instability), and urine DNA methylation biomarkers (epigenetic state), will modulate the strength of this interaction by altering antigen presentation and immune recognition. This yields testable predictions: (1) among TIL-high tumors, MMR-proficient cases show higher therapy_response rates than MMR-deficient cases; (2) among MMR-proficient tumors, higher clonal neoantigen load correlates with better response; (3) secondary modifiers shift the magnitude of the TIL/MMR interaction. The endpoints will be objective therapy_response metrics (e.g., RECIST-based ORR/longitudinal progression-free survival) in cohorts treated with anti-PD-1/PD-L1 agents.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
