# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), high PFKFB3-driven glycolytic reprogramming promotes cancer stem cell–like properties (Stem.Sig) preferentially in tumors with low stromal-to-tumor ratio (low TSR). This effect is potentiated by EPB41L2-associated cytoskeletal/adhesion remodeling that reconfigures the tumor microenvironment to support stem-like cells. Consequently, HCC tumors that simultaneously meet: (i) low TSR, (ii) high PFKFB3 expression, and (iii) a high EPB41L2 risk signature will exhibit elevated Stem.Sig activity and poorer overall survival (OS) than tumors lacking this metabolic–stromal interplay. The interaction is at least partly mediated through mTOR–linked growth and adhesion/ECM remodeling pathways, suggesting that disrupting PFKFB3-driven glycolysis or EPB41L2-dependent adhesion could mitigate stemness and improve prognosis in this subset.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** drives stemness (Stem.Sig), modulated by the microenvironment (EPB41L2 and TSR) and amplified via mTOR signaling. This yields a testable prognostic frame: in HCC, the combination of low TSR, high PFKFB3, and elevated EPB41L2 risk signature should prognosticate worse OS due to enhanced stemness and niche support.

**Prediction 2:** In retrospective cohort data, the prognostic benefit of the imaging feature will be modified by the anchor gene expression level (significant interaction term).


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.

## Caveats & Risks

- Gene expression does not equal protein activity; phospho-protein or functional assays are needed for confirmation.

## Alternative Directions

- Cancer stemness (Stem.Sig): Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
- stroma content: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
