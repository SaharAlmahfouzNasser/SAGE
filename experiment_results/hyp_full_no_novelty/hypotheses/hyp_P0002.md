# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlights that immune-infiltrated MIBC (signals from bTMB/PD-L1/S100A9 and immune-score analogs) and genomic instability markers (MSH6) do not uniformly predict favorable outcomes or immunotherapy benefit — why do some TIL-rich tumors fail to respond to checkpoint blockade? 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on (a) MSH6 as an independent prognostic/DNA-repair axis (Tier1 Core Path 2) because MSH6 status alters mutation/neoantigen load and immune recruitment; and (b) the S100A9 → PD-L1 → bTMB inflammatory/immune-suppression axis (Tier1 Core Path 3) because S100A9-mediated myeloid inflammation can enforce immune suppression despite lymphocyte presence. These two provide a direct mechanistic tension (neoantigen-driven TIL recruitment versus myeloid-driven suppression). 3) Tier 2 evidence supporting the claim: Immunoscore/ TIL-based frameworks (supporting path 2) validate that spatial immune metrics matter; GARS1 and systemic metabolic panels suggest tumor-intrinsic and host-state modifiers that could shape myeloid inflammation or T-cell function; AI-accessibility supports feasibility of extracting robust TIL density from H&E for large cohorts. 4) Why therapy_response was chosen as the endpoint: The core mechanisms (MSH6-driven mutational load and S100A9-driven immunosuppression) directly inform sensitivity or resistance to PD-1/PD-L1 checkpoint blockade — a clinically actionable therapy response proxy most relevant to these pathways. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded urine-DNA methylation biomarkers and broad metabolic markers from the core hypothesis to keep focus on the immune/repair interaction; those remain in secondary_modifiers for follow-up integration. I also excluded PD-L1 as a primary anchor to avoid redundancy (S100A9 mechanistically links to PD-L1 and will be tested alongside PD-L1 in secondary analyses)?

## Working Hypothesis

In muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), the predictive value of intratumoral TIL density for response to PD-1/PD-L1 blockade is governed by the interaction of 1) MSH6 status (MMR competence vs deficiency) and 2) S100A9-driven myeloid inflammation. Specifically: (i) high TIL density combined with intact MSH6 and low S100A9 yields the highest likelihood of objective response to PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors; (ii) MSH6 deficiency or high S100A9 expression diminishes the predictive value of TIL density, leading to reduced response despite abundant TILs; (iii) TIL density alone is insufficient across the full spectrum of MSH6 and S100A9 statuses. The strongest predictive model should include an interaction term TIL_density × MSH6_status × S100A9_level, with further refinement by PD-L1 expression and bTMB. This hypothesis will be tested by retrospective analysis of treated cohorts and validated with spatial immune profiling and functional assays in vitro and in vivo.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** retrospective cohort analysis of MIBC patients treated with PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors evaluating TIL density, MSH6 IHC/sequence-based status, S100A9 expression, PD-L1, and bTMB with models incorporating interaction terms;

**Prediction 2:** multiplex immunofluorescence or imaging mass cytometry to map TILs relative to S100A9+ myeloid cells and PD-L1 expression;

**Prediction 3:** in vitro co-culture of bladder cancer cells with defined MSH6 status and S100A9 exposure to assess T cell activation;


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
