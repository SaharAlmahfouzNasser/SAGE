# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

NMIBC shows heterogeneity in BCG (intravesical immunotherapy) benefit—some tumors with dense immune infiltration nonetheless recur, while others with low apparent local immunity respond; why do urine epigenetic signals and systemic mutational load modulate this discordance? 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on (a) urine DNA methylation biomarkers as a non‑invasive readout of tumor epigenetic programs that can suppress immune visibility (Core Path 1) and (b) bTMB as a systemic, non‑invasive proxy for neoantigen burden that can drive adaptive immune activation (Core Path 3). I included TIL density (imaging feature) to capture local immune context (ties conceptually to Core Path 2/Immunoscore). 3) Tier 2 supporting evidence: TP53 and ER status can shape both mutation burden and epigenetic landscapes (modifying methylation and TMB); Immunoscore concepts validate that spatial immune context matters; TMB (tissue) and stool/urine methylation panels (SDC2/TFPI2) support cross‑tissue epigenetic biomarker translatability. 4) Why therapy_response (BCG): the most actionable clinical problem in NMIBC is predicting/optimizing response to BCG; available non‑invasive readouts (urine methylation, bTMB) are well suited as pre‑treatment stratifiers for intravesical therapy. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded proposing specific antigen‑presentation genes or additional molecular features in the core hypothesis to keep focus to exactly one imaging metric + ≤2 molecular anchors; Tier‑2 entities (TP53, ER status, Immunoscore, tissue TMB, SDC2/TFPI2) are listed as secondary_modifiers for follow‑up mechanistic testing rather than included in the primary claim?

## Working Hypothesis

In NMIBC, high intratumoral TIL density does not predict favorable response to BCG when pre-treatment urine DNA methylation signatures indicate tumor immune-visibility silencing, whereas elevated systemic neoantigen burden (bTMB) can counteract this silencing by promoting neoantigen-driven T cell activation. Therefore, pre-treatment integration of TIL density, urine methylation biomarkers, and bTMB will stratify NMIBC patients by likelihood of BCG response, guiding decisions toward adjunctive immunotherapy for those unlikely to respond.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
