# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Intravesical BCG provokes local immune recruitment but also up-regulates PD-L1; why do some immune-infiltrated (TLS/TIL-rich) urothelial cancers nevertheless fail to respond to BCG? 2) Tier 1 paths used: the chain linking PI3-kinase pathway activation → PD-L1 and the BCG → PD-L1 induction; I represent PI3K activation by PIK3CA (tumor-intrinsic driver) and PD-L1 by CD274 because these directly explain constitutive versus therapy-induced PD-L1. 3) Tier 2/supporting evidence: TMPRSS2/ERG and PSMA (FOLH1) appear in the path as upstream oncogenic/contextual modifiers that could alter immune phenotype or cross-talk between tumor types; they support exploring whether oncogenic programs rewire PD-L1 regulation. 4) Endpoint choice: therapy_response (BCG response) is the most relevant clinical question for this chain, since the path centers on BCG-induced immune modulation and PD-L1-driven immune suppression. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded broader pathway descriptors (e.g., generic 'PI3K pathway' label) in favor of a measurable gene (PIK3CA) and omitted additional exploratory genes (e.g., other immune checkpoints, metabolic regulators) to keep the hypothesis sharply testable and within the 1 imaging + 2 gene constraint?

## Working Hypothesis

TLS-rich urothelial carcinomas with tumor-intrinsic PI3K activation driven by PIK3CA alterations sustain constitutive CD274 (PD-L1) expression within the tumor microenvironment, establishing a PD-L1–dominant immunosuppressive niche that dampens BCG-induced immune recruitment and activation. Consequently, TLS-high tumors with PIK3CA-driven PD-L1 upregulation will exhibit poorer clinical response to intravesical BCG compared with TLS-high tumors lacking this PI3K/PD-L1 axis activation. Secondary modifiers—TMPRSS2/ERG fusion status and FOLH1 (PSMA)–related signaling—may further influence PD-L1 levels or TLS maturation and thereby modulate the magnitude of BCG response in TLS-rich tumors. Imaging-feature TLS-like aggregates will serve as a phenotypic readout linking tumor-intrinsic PI3K activity to the immunosuppressive TLS niche and BCG responsiveness.
