## Working Hypothesis

In bladder tumors with high stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR), elevated tumor cell PROS1 expression promotes paracrine signaling within the stromal compartment via TAM receptor–mediated pathways, driving tumor cell proliferation as indicated by increased MKI67 expression. If this TSR-dependent PROS1–Ki-67 axis operates, TSR-high/PROS1-high tumors will have shorter progression-free interval (PFI) compared with TSR-high/PROS1-low or TSR-low groups.
