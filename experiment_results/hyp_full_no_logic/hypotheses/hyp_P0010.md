# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

Among muscle-invasive bladder cancers (MIBC), TLS-like lymphoid aggregates are present but fail to support productive anti-tumor immunity when the tumor microenvironment exhibits high TGF-β signaling in conjunction with SARIFA-driven tumor–adipocyte/stroma remodeling that promotes immune exclusion. Consequently, TLS-rich tumors with a high TGF-β/SARIFA fibrogenic/adipose-stromal niche will show reduced response to immune checkpoint blockade compared with TLS-rich tumors lacking this remodeling (and with a favorable Immunoscore).

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** , and SARIFA presence; spatially resolved profiling (imaging mass cytometry, multiplex IHC, spatial transcriptomics) to map immune cell states and exclusion zones; functional in vitro systems (organoids co-cultured with adipocytes, matrices with TGF-β–driven fibrosis) to test reversibility with TGF-β blockade; and in vivo models to assess whether combining TGF-β inhibitors with anti-PD-1/PD-L1 improves responses specifically in TLS-rich SARIFA+ tumors.

**Prediction 2:** In retrospective cohort data, the prognostic benefit of the imaging feature will be modified by the anchor gene expression level (significant interaction term).

**Prediction 3:** At the tissue level, tumors with the imaging feature but low anchor gene activity will show immune cells confined to structural peripheries rather than infiltrating tumor nests.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.
- Multi-modal validation pipeline: computational -> spatial -> functional, providing mechanistic depth beyond association.

## Caveats & Risks

- Hypothesis is computationally derived and requires experimental validation.
