# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In NMIBC patients treated with intravesical BCG, TLS abundance and organization predict favorable therapy response only when tumor cells are MSH6-proficient and ARNP/ADNP transcriptional activity in circulating tumor cells (CTCs) is low. MSH6 deficiency and/or high ADNP-driven transcriptional programs in tumor cells drive immune evasion and remodeling of neoantigen landscapes, diminishing the protective effect of TLS. Therefore, TLS alone is an insufficient predictor of BCG response in the context of an MSH6-deficient and/or high-ADNP state. A composite biomarker integrating (i) TLS density/architecture from histology, (ii) MSH6 status (IHC and/or sequencing for MMR defects), and (iii) ADNP ctRNA level from CTCs will more accurately stratify NMIBC patients by BCG response, outperforming TLS alone. Secondary modifiers (PD-L1/CD274 expression, TP53 status, tumor mutational burden, estrogen receptor status, and systemic HDL-C) will modulate the strength of the TLS–MSH6–ADNP relationship and improve predictive performance when incorporated. Predictions include: (a) TLS-positive, MSH6-proficient, ADNP-low patients will show highest BCG response rates and longest relapse-free survival; (b) TLS-positive but MSH6-deficient or ADNP-high patients will exhibit reduced response despite TLS presence; (c) a three-way interaction TLS × MSH6 × ADNP will significantly predict response better than TLS alone; (d) the composite score will generalize across prospective NMIBC cohorts and guide earlier escalation to alternative therapies for non-responders.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
