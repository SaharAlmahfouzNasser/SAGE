# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In bladder cancer, MSH6 deficiency drives MSI-high status and elevates neoantigen burden, creating substrate for T cell–mediated recognition. However, an tumor-intrinsic translational program governed by eIF4E activity modulates the capacity to present antigens and to establish TLS-like organized lymphoid structures. Specifically, high eIF4E activity reprograms the tumor secretome and antigen-presentation machinery to reduce TLS initiation/maturation and dampen pro–TLS chemokine signaling (e.g., CXCL13, CCL19/CCL21) while potentially enhancing immune-evasive signals, leading to poor TLS architecture, diminished T cell infiltration, and reduced responsiveness to immune checkpoint blockade (ICB). Conversely, low eIF4E activity in MSH6-deficient/MSI tumors supports robust TLS formation and maturation, enabling effective T cell priming and a greater likelihood of response to PD-1/PD-L1 blockade. Therefore, TLS imaging alone will not reliably predict ICB response unless considered in combination with MSH6/MSI status and eIF4E activity; the subgroup with TLS-rich, MSH6-deficient tumors and low eIF4E activity will show the strongest therapy_response, while TLS-rich tumors with high eIF4E activity will exhibit attenuated responses. This framework predicts that integrating TLS metrics with MSH6/MSI and eIF4E status will yield a more accurate predictive model than TLS or MSH6/MSI status alone.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
