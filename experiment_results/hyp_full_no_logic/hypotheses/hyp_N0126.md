# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In breast cancer, TP53 mutation promotes EMT via upregulation of EMT transcription factors that suppress CDH1 (E-cadherin), driving remodeling of the tumor stroma and resulting in a high stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR). This TSR–driven stromal expansion reflects EMT-associated desmoplasia and diminished epithelial integrity, and the prognostic impact of TSR is most pronounced when CDH1 is downregulated in TP53-mutant tumors. Clinically, TSR-high tumors with low CDH1 expression and TP53 mutation will have worse overall survival compared with TSR-high tumors with intact CDH1 and/or wild-type TP53. This relationship may be further modulated by immune-contexture, as reflected by Tier 2 modifiers such as LAG3 and another EMT/immunity-related gene, which could amplify or mitigate the TSR-associated risk by shaping the tumor microenvironment. The central test will be to demonstrate a significant TP53/CDH1–TSR interaction predicting OS in breast cancer, with replication across independent datasets and supportive mechanistic evidence from cell/in vivo models showing TP53 loss promotes EMT and reduces CDH1, contributing to stromal expansion and adverse outcome.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
