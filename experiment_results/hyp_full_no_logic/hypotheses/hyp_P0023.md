# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

## Working Hypothesis

Among non–muscle-invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC) patients, tumors with a high stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) that also show elevated TGFB1 signaling will exhibit a fibrotic ECM and an immunosuppressive microenvironment characterized by reduced CD8A+ T cell infiltration or activity within tumor compartments. This TGFB1–CAF/ECM axis mediates immune exclusion and promotes tumor progression, resulting in a shorter progression-free interval (PFI). The association is strongest in TSR-high tumors with high TGFB1 and low CD8A expression; in these tumors, secondary modifiers (urine DNA methylation, tumor mutational burden, circulating tumor cells [CTCs], E-FABP, CDKN2A status, HDL-C) reflect overall aggressiveness and metastatic potential but do not alone drive the core mechanistic pathway. The model predicts: (1) TSR-high/TGFB1-high/CD8A-low tumors have the worst PFI; (2) spatial profiling will reveal CD8+ T cell exclusion from tumor nests and intact/aged stroma; (3) disrupting TGFB1 signaling or CAF–ECM remodeling will enhance CD8A+ T cell localization and function and improve PFI; (4) urinary and CTC-based modifiers will correlate with risk but not abolish the TGFB1–stroma–CD8A axis.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
