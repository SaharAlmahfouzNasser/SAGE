# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist framed a core question: why do measures of immune infiltration (Immunoscore/TILs) not consistently predict favorable outcomes or therapy response in bladder cancer, and how does tumor genomic instability (MSH6 status) modulate that relationship? 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on Immunoscore (immune-context/prognostic biomarker) and MSH6 (MMR gene driving mutation burden). ## Working Hypothesis

Does the combination of MSH6 deficiency and high Tumor-Infiltrating Lymphocyte (TIL) density (as captured by Immunoscore) predict reduced or nonoptimal response to immune checkpoint blockade in bladder cancer, due to MSH6-driven neoantigen heterogeneity promoting dysfunctional/exhausted T cell responses despite high infiltration? If so, does adding Immunoscore to TIL density improve the accuracy of predicting therapy_response compared with TIL density alone, thereby enabling better stratification for combination therapies (e.g., targeting TGF-β or other suppressive pathways) in MSH6-deficient, TIL-high tumors?
