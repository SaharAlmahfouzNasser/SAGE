# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Tier 1 synthesis highlights apparent discordance between tumor-intrinsic genomic signals (MSH6-driven DNA repair status) and microenvironmental/epithelial–stromal programs (TGF-β–driven fibrosis/EMT and urine DNA methylation readouts). The key puzzle is: under what microenvironmental conditions does a tumor's DNA-repair–linked immunogenicity (or lack thereof) translate into altered progression risk in NMIBC? 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on the TGFB1 (TGF-β) axis because it mechanistically links to ECM remodeling and stromal expansion (directly mapable to stromal-to-tumor ratio on H&E). I also used MSH6 because mismatch-repair defects alter neoantigen load and prognostic behavior in bladder cancer. These two provide a clear tumor–microenvironment interaction to test. 3) Tier 2 evidence that supports the claim: TMB/neoantigen context and CTC dynamics inform systemic tumor burden and immune visibility; urine DNA methylation biomarkers give a non-invasive tumor burden readout that can stratify shedding vs tissue-confined disease; inflammation-linked markers (pZVR) and AI-accessible MSI signatures provide orthogonal measures of immune suppression and biomarker accessibility. Together they supply testable modifiers of the central interaction. 4) Endpoint choice: I selected progression-free interval (PFI) because NMIBC clinical decision-making hinges on risk of progression to muscle-invasive disease; PFI is the most relevant, sensitive endpoint for mechanistic hypotheses about tumor–stroma–immune interplay. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded placing urine DNA methylation biomarkers in the 1–2 anchor slots to obey the imaging+genes constraint and to keep the mechanistic claim sharp (imaging feature + molecular anchors). Exploratory/less directly mechanistic signals (e.g., SARIFA, E-FABP, PSA cross-cancer links) were omitted from the core hypothesis and relegated to secondary_modifiers or left out because they are less bladder-specific or mechanistically diffuse?

## Working Hypothesis

In non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC), a high stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) driven by TGFB1 signaling creates an immunosuppressive extracellular matrix that diminishes the immunogenic potential of MSH6-deficient neoantigens. Consequently, tumors with TSR-high and MSH6 deficiency will exhibit shorter progression-free interval (PFI) than TSR-low or MSH6-proficient tumors. This effect is amplified when TGFB1 signaling is active, such that the TSR-high/MSH6-deficient group shows reduced cytotoxic T cell activity and a more suppressive microenvironment (e.g., higher Tregs, M2-like macrophages, and dampened CD8+ T cell spatial engagement) compared to other groups. The hypothesis remains focused on TSR as the imaging feature and TGFB1/MSH6 as the anchor genes; urine DNA methylation biomarkers are considered a secondary modifier and not part of the core testable hypothesis.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** In retrospective cohort data, the prognostic benefit of the imaging feature will be modified by the anchor gene expression level (significant interaction term).

**Prediction 2:** At the tissue level, tumors with the imaging feature but low anchor gene activity will show immune cells confined to structural peripheries rather than infiltrating tumor nests.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
