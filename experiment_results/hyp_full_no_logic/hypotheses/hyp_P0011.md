# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

TLS-like lymphoid aggregates confer sensitivity to gemcitabine plus PD-L1 blockade only when the tumor microenvironment supports a robust IFNG-driven transcriptional program that upregulates antigen presentation (MHC I/II) and strengthens CD8+ T cell cytotoxic activity. Consequently, TLS-rich tumors with high IFNG signaling will exhibit higher therapy_response to gemcitabine + anti-PD-L1 than TLS-rich tumors with low IFNG signaling. In parallel, intrinsic invasion programs and an immunosuppressive myeloid milieu can dampen or override IFNG-driven responses, leading to early relapse despite an initial response. Therefore, a biologically defined responder subset is determined by (i) TLS presence and maturation, (ii) sufficient IFNG activity, and (iii) competent antigen presentation, whereas high-grade/invasive phenotypes and hostile myeloid environments set a threshold for durability. The hypothesis implies testable predictions: (a) among TLS-rich tumors, therapy_response correlates positively with an IFNG activity score and MHC I/II expression; (b) disrupting IFNG signaling abrogates the TLS-dependent response; (c) enhancing IFNG signaling or antigen presentation in TLS-rich, IFNG-low tumors converts them toward sensitivity; (d) invasion-program signatures predict early relapse even when initial response is observed; (e) PD-L1 expression alone does not predict response without concurrent IFNG-driven antigen presentation.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** At the tissue level, tumors with the imaging feature but low anchor gene activity will show immune cells confined to structural peripheries rather than infiltrating tumor nests.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
