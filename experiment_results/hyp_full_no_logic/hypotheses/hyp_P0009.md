# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC) tumors that are MSI/dMMR and contain TLS-like lymphoid aggregates, antitumor immunity and response to immune checkpoint blockade (ICI) are governed by tumor-intrinsic pyrimidine metabolism via DPYD. Specifically, high DPYD activity creates a pyrimidine metabolite milieu that supports TLS maturation, dendritic cell antigen presentation, and effector T-cell function, leading to robust ICI responses. In contrast, DPYD downregulation or low activity leads to accumulation of pyrimidine metabolites that suppress TLS maturation and antigen presentation, resulting in poor ICI responses despite high neoantigen burden. Thus, DPYD expression/activity functions as a functional modifier of the TLS–neoantigen axis and predicts ICI outcomes beyond TLS presence or MSI status alone. Testable predictions include: (1) DPYD expression positively correlates with TLS maturity markers and ICI responsiveness in TLS-rich MSI/dMMR MIBC; (2) driving DPYD expression or activity in MSI/dMMR bladder cancer models enhances TLS formation/function and improves ICI efficacy, whereas DPYD inhibition diminishes these features; (3) metabolomic profiling will reveal distinct pyrimidine metabolite signatures that distinguish DPYD-high vs DPYD-low TLS+ tumors in terms of immunologic competence.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.

## Caveats & Risks

- Gene expression does not equal protein activity; phospho-protein or functional assays are needed for confirmation.

## Alternative Directions

- TP53: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
