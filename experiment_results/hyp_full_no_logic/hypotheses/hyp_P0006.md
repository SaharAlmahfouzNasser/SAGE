# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist's synthesis asks why DNA-repair–deficiency signals (MSI/dMMR, MSH6) and measures of tumor immune organization (Immunoscore) do not consistently translate into improved outcomes or therapy benefit in MIBC — specifically, under what conditions histologic immune structures (TLS/Immunoscore) fail to yield productive anti-tumor immunity. 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on the MSI/dMMR axis (Core Path 1) and the MSH6 prognostic signal (Core Path 2) together with the immune-contexture axis (Immunoscore, Core Path 3) because these three directly frame a mechanistic tension: mismatch-repair–driven neoantigen generation versus the ability of local immune architecture to prime effective T cells. 3) Tier 2 evidence supporting the claim: noninvasive methylation markers and metastasis predictors (urine-DNA methylation biomarkers, SDC2/TFPI2, pZVR) provide orthogonal ways to identify tumor subgroups and clinical risk that could modulate or confound responses; metabolic/endocrine markers (HDL-C/IGF1 panel) suggest systemic contexts that might influence immune competence. These support testing whether immune–DNA repair interactions are dependent on tumor-intrinsic or host-level modifiers. 4) Why therapy_response as endpoint: the translational question centers on why immune organization (TLS/Immunoscore) does not reliably predict benefit from immune checkpoint blockade in MIBC — a therapy-response endpoint is the most direct, actionable clinical readout to resolve this mechanistic puzzle. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded detailed antigen-presentation genes (e.g., HLA locus), proliferation markers, and SARIFA/pZVR mechanistic speculation from the core hypothesis to keep the claim sharp and focused on the DNA-repair ↔ immune-architecture interaction; those elements are listed as secondary_modifiers for follow-up analyses?

## Working Hypothesis

In muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), TLS-like lymphoid aggregates are associated with improved response to immune checkpoint blockade (ICB) only when tumors retain proficient MSH6-mediated mismatch repair (MMR). In tumors with MSH6 alterations leading to MSI/dMMR, TLS presence does not predict enhanced therapy_response and may correlate with reduced durability of response due to mutational/neoantigen heterogeneity and potential impairment of effective antigen presentation. Therefore, there is a significant interaction: TLS_status × MSH6/MSI_status that governs ICB outcomes. Clinically, TLS-high/MMR-proficient tumors are expected to show higher objective response rates and longer response durability to ICIs, whereas TLS-high MSI/dMMR tumors driven by MSH6 loss may exhibit attenuated or transient benefit. This framework yields testable predictions for stratified analyses and informs combination strategies in TLS-positive/MSI-dMMR contexts.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Retrospective cohorts of MIBC patients treated with ICIs with archived tumor samples for TLS assessment (immunohistochemistry/IF for TLS markers) and MSH6/MSI status (IHC for MSH6, MSI-PCR/NGS).

**Prediction 2:** Stratify by TLS_high vs TLS_low and by MSH6 status/MSI/dMMR; model therapy_response (ORR, DCR, PFS) with an interaction term TLS_status × MSH6/MSI_status; adjust for tumor mutational burden, PD-L1, stage, and prior therapies.

**Prediction 3:** Validate with a prospective cohort and apply spatial transcriptomics or multiplex immunofluorescence to characterize TLS maturity, antigen presentation pathways, and T cell clonality within TLS zones across MSH6/MSI contexts.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
