# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

TSR is a simple, reproducible H&E biomarker for stromal abundance, but it is unclear whether and how high stromal fraction causally drives poorer outcomes in MIBC versus acting as a sampling/confounding feature. The ontologist's single-path analysis frames TSR as a readout of desmoplasia, ECM remodeling, altered nutrient/oxygen diffusion, and local immune modulation?

## Working Hypothesis

Primary question: In muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), does a high stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) measured on H&E sections independently predict poorer overall survival (OS) after controlling for standard clinico-pathologic factors and treatment? If true, the mechanism is that desmoplastic stroma (reflected by high TSR) increases interstitial pressure and ECM density, reducing chemotherapy penetration and promoting hypoxic, immunosuppressive microenvironments, thereby lowering treatment efficacy and enabling tumor progression. This yields a testable prediction: high TSR will be associated with worse OS independent of stage and grade, and this association will persist across plausible sampling strategies (e.g., including the invasive front but excluding necrotic regions). Secondary predictions include: (a) TSR remains prognostic when perioperative chemotherapy is considered, suggesting a chemo-penetration/biophysical basis; (b) the TSR–OS association is not fully explained by tumor stage or grade, supporting a mechanistic imaging biomarker role. Anchor genes remain an empty array due to data constraints, consistent with a purely imaging-driven mechanistic hypothesis. Overall, the hypothesis is structured to be testable via multivariable survival analyses and robustness checks across sampling strategies, with TSR serving as a potential marker to identify patients who may benefit from stroma-targeting or intensified systemic therapy.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
