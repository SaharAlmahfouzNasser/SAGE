# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The graph links PTEN status to prognosis and separately links LAG3 to prognosis, suggesting a tension where immune infiltration (TILs) does not always yield better outcomes; mechanistically, PTEN loss activates PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling which could reprogram the tumor microenvironment to favor T-cell dysfunction (LAG3-mediated exhaustion). The key question becomes: why do some TIL-rich tumors still have poor survival, and could PTEN-driven signaling explain elevated LAG3-related immune suppression that nullifies TIL benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In breast cancer, PTEN loss leads to heightened PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway activity that reshapes the tumor microenvironment toward immunosuppression. When intratumoral TIL density is high, concurrent high LAG3 expression indicates T-cell exhaustion, which dampens the beneficial prognostic effect of TILs. Therefore, PTEN-deficient, TIL-high, LAG3-high tumors will have worse overall survival than PTEN-intact, TIL-high, LAG3-low tumors. The hypothesis remains testable across TCGA-BRCA data and can be interrogated for potential therapeutic synergy with mTOR pathway inhibition and/or LAG3 blockade, with TP53 and mTORC1 status acting as secondary modifiers that influence the strength of the PTEN–LAG3–TIL interaction.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
