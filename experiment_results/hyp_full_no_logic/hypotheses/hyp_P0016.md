# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

Primary hypothesis: In NMIBC treated with intravesical BCG, TLS-like lymphoid aggregates do not uniformly predict a durable response when PD-L1 is upregulated (adaptive resistance) and when tumor mutational burden (bTMB) is low. Specifically, TLS-high tumors with PD-L1-low and high bTMB will exhibit higher rates of durable response than TLS-high tumors with PD-L1-high and/or low bTMB, and TLS-low tumors will have poorer responses irrespective of PD-L1/bTMB status. Mechanistically, PD-L1 upregulation within TLS-rich NMIBC dampens TLS-primed effector T cells, an effect amplified by low neoantigen burden (low bTMB). Conversely, TLS-high, PD-L1-low, and high bTMB tumors foster robust, durable anti-tumor T-cell activity and better BCG outcomes. Translationally, a triple biomarker phenotype—TLS-high + PD-L1-high + low bTMB—identifies patients least likely to benefit from BCG alone and who may require early addition of PD-1/PD-L1 blockade or alternative immunotherapeutic strategies.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** quantify TLS-like aggregates by histology/digital pathology,

**Prediction 2:** measure PD-L1 expression by IHC in tumor and immune compartments, and

**Prediction 3:** derive bTMB from a validated targeted sequencing panel. Outcomes include: therapy_response at 3–6 months (complete response), and durable response metrics (recurrence-free survival at 12 months).


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Caveats & Risks
