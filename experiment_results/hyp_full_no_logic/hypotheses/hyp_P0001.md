# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), high tumor budding at the invasive front reflects an active epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EMT) program as evidenced by an elevated EMT gene signature; this EMT activation mediates enhanced local invasion and early dissemination, partially driving worse overall survival independent of conventional pathologic stage and nodal status. Consequently, tumors with high TB and high EMT_signature constitute a high-risk subgroup with prognostic information beyond stage, and the TB→EMT→invasion axis represents a mechanistic link between histology and outcome in MIBC.


| Element | Value |

| Imaging feature | Tumor budding at the invasive front |
| Anchor gene(s) | EMT gene signature |
| Primary endpoint | OS |

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Retrospective cohort analysis: assemble MIBC patients with available histopathology to score TB and with transcriptomic data to compute EMT_signature scores; perform multivariate Cox models adjusting for stage, nodal status, grade, and other relevant covariates.

**Prediction 2:** Interaction/mediation analysis: test whether EMT_signature mediates the TB–OS association and quantify the proportion mediated.

**Prediction 3:** Validation at protein level: in a subset, perform immunohistochemistry for epithelial vs mesenchymal markers (e.g., loss of epithelial adhesion and gain of mesenchymal traits) to corroborate the EMT state associated with TB.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.

## Caveats & Risks

- Hypothesis is computationally derived and requires experimental validation.

## Alternative Directions

- Tumor stage (pT/pN): Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
- Lymphovascular invasion: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
