# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

## Working Hypothesis

In MSH6-deficient bladder cancers, a high stromal-to-tumor ratio driven by TGF-β signaling creates a fibrotic, immunosuppressive microenvironment that blunts neoantigen–specific T cell responses despite a high mutational/neoantigen burden. Clinically, this TSR-high, TGF-β–active, MSH6-deficient phenotype will exhibit reduced response to immune checkpoint blockade compared with TSR-low tumors, and this poor response may be improved by combining anti–TGF-β–targeted therapy with immunotherapy.
