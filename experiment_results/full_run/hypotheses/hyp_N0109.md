# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Why do some tumors with favorable immune architecture still fail to achieve better clinical outcomes?

## Working Hypothesis

Imaging feature: Tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte density. Anchor genes: LZTS2 and CD44. Endpoint: overall survival (OS). Primary mechanistic claim: In breast cancer, the OS benefit of high TIL density is conditional on LZTS2 promoter hypomethylation and preserved LZTS2 function, which suppresses EMT and permits CD8+ T cells to effectively target CD44-expressing tumor cells. In tumors with LZTS2 hypermethylation (silencing), EMT is potentiated and the immune clearance by CD8+ T cells is impaired, attenuating or negating the TIL-associated OS advantage even when TIL density is high. A secondary modifier, RASSF1A methylation status, modulates this axis and can further influence EMT propensity and immune susceptibility, refining the TIL–LZTS2–CD44 interaction and its impact on OS.

| --- | --- |
| Imaging feature | Tumor-Infiltrating Lymphocyte (TIL) density |
| Anchor gene(s) | LZTS2, CD44 |

# 2. Why This Gene — Not Others

## Primary hypothesis gene(s)

**LZTS2:** LZTS2 methylation → LZTS2 function → inhibition of EMT (EMT is the mechanistic bottleneck), and the CD8+/CD44 → survival link; I built on these because they connect tumor-intrinsic epigenetic state to both phenotype (EMT/CD44) and immune context (CD8+/TILs).

**CD44:** CD44 associate with better OS, yet immune infiltration does not always translate into survival benefit — the path suggests epigenetic regulation of LZTS2 (lower methylation) restrains EMT and may determine when immune presence is effective.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** retrospective cohort analyses (TCGA/METABRIC) with methylation, expression, TIL scoring, and survival data;

**Prediction 2:** epigenetic editing in breast cancer cell lines to modulate LZTS2 methylation and assess EMT and CD8+ cytotoxicity in co-culture;

**Prediction 3:** xenograft or humanized mouse models to evaluate TIL-driven control of CD44+ cells under different LZTS2/RASSF1A statuses;


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.

## Caveats & Risks

- Gene expression does not equal protein activity; phospho-protein or functional assays are needed for confirmation.

## Alternative Directions

- RASSF1A: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
