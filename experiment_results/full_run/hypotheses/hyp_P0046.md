# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

Mechanistic: In HER2-positive tumors with TLS-like lymphoid aggregates, high tumor-derived CHI3L1 drives a PD-L1–dominant, immunosuppressive state that impairs antigen presentation and T cell priming within TLS, thereby limiting productive anti-tumor immunity and reducing response to HER2-targeted therapy and PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors. Clinical: Assessing TLS presence together with CHI3L1 and PD-L1 (CD274) status will identify patients who exhibit discordant immune architecture (TLS present but functionally suppressed) and have poorer therapy response; these patients may benefit from combination strategies targeting CHI3L1 or PD-L1 pathways in addition to HER2-targeted therapy. Secondary modifiers (CD8A, mtDNA_content, SUVmax, CPS, MUC1, LINE-1 methylation) will serve as orthogonal readouts to refine risk stratification and mechanistic interpretation, and to predict which patients are most likely to convert benefit with combination regimens.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Retrospective cohort analysis to correlate TLS presence, CHI3L1, PD-L1, and secondary modifiers with therapy response in HER2+ patients treated with HER2-targeted therapy ± immunotherapy.

**Prediction 2:** Spatial multi-omic studies to assess CHI3L1-driven PD-L1 expression within TLS micro-niches and its impact on T cell activation markers.

**Prediction 3:** In vitro/in vivo models to test whether CHI3L1 blockade or combined CHI3L1/PD-L1 inhibition improves T cell priming in TLS-like structures.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
