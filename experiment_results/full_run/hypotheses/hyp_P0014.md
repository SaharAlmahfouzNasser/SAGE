## Working Hypothesis

TLS-like lymphoid aggregates in NMIBC generally reflect organized local anti-tumor immunity, but when tumors harbor MSH6 deficiency, this TLS presence may not translate into BCG responsiveness because MSH6 loss drives MMR deficiency and hypermutation that upregulates PD-L1 and promotes T cell exhaustion, decoupling TLS from effective intravesical immunotherapy. Therefore, NMIBC tumors with TLS and MSH6 deficiency plus high PD-L1 expression will show reduced therapy_response to BCG, identifying patients who may require PD-1/PD-L1–directed or alternative systemic or combination immunotherapies rather than standard intravesical treatment.

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)

- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
