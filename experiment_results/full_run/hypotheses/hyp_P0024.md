# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Why do some NMIBC tumors with organized local immune structures (TLS/TLS-like aggregates) still fail to respond to intravesical BCG? The Ontologist synthesis highlights three converging signals: BCG-driven local immunity (clinical trigger), noninvasive tumor-derived transcripts (ADNP mRNA/CTCs) as indicators of tumor state, and DNA repair status (MSH6) that shapes neoantigen landscapes and immune engagement. 2) Tier 1 paths used: Core Path 1 (BCG → immune activation/biomarker readouts) and Core Path 3 (ADNP mRNA detected in liquid biopsies) provide the immune activation and tumor-derived-transcript axes; Core Path 2 (urine DNA methylation) motivated noninvasive monitoring but was deprioritized for the core mechanistic claim. I selected MSH6 and ADNP because MSH6 directly modulates tumor antigenicity/immune visibility and ADNP represents a measurable tumor-derived transcript linked to tumor-intrinsic programs that could impair immune function. 3) Tier 2 evidence supporting the claim: TP53, MSI status, ER-context and Immunoscore-like immune contexture support mechanisms by which tumor-intrinsic genomic instability and immune state modulate the functional competence of TLS and response to BCG — e.g., MSI/MSH6 alterations alter neoantigen load and TP53/ER signaling can shape epithelial programs that affect immune-evasion. 4) Why therapy_response was chosen as the endpoint: The core clinical lever in NMIBC is response to intravesical BCG (treatment selection and monitoring); therapy_response is the most direct, actionable endpoint for testing how immune architecture and tumor molecular state interact. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded urine DNA-methylation panels and broad cross-cancer biomarkers (e.g., stool methylation, HDL-C) from the core hypothesis to keep the mechanistic question sharp; Immunoscore and MSI/TP53/ER were moved to secondary_modifiers for follow-up stratified analyses rather than being overloaded into the primary claim?

## Working Hypothesis

TLS-like lymphoid aggregates confer functional anti-tumor immunity to intravesical BCG in NMIBC only when the tumor resides in a favorable intrinsic state: intact MSH6-driven mismatch repair that sustains a robust neoantigen landscape and low tumor ADNP expression that does not suppress antigen presentation or TLS activation. Under MSH6 deficiency or elevated ADNP, despite TLS presence, tumor-intrinsic programs dampen immune visibility and/or effector activation, leading to reduced therapy_response. Clinically, TLS-high NMIBC with MSH6 intact and low ADNP mRNA will show higher response rates to BCG; TLS-high with MSH6 loss or high ADNP will exhibit poorer responses and may require alternative or combination strategies. TLS-negative tumors are unlikely to achieve robust responses regardless of MSH6/ADNP status. Secondary modifiers (TP53 status, ESR1/ER signaling, MSI, and Immunoscore) will modulate the magnitude of TLS-driven responses but are not sufficient alone to predict outcome without the core triad.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** TLS structures provide localized adaptive immune niches but require adequate antigenic stimulation and immune activation to function;

**Prediction 2:** MSH6-maintained mismatch repair shapes neoantigen burden and interferon/antigen presentation pathways, influencing tumor visibility to T cells;

**Prediction 3:** ADNP, as a chromatin regulator, can reprogram transcriptional programs that affect antigen processing/presentation and immunomodulatory gene expression. Together, these factors can explain why TLS presence alone fails to guarantee BCG response in NMIBC.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
