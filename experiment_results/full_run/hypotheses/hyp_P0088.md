## Clinical Question

## Working Hypothesis

High HTAV indicates spatially structured tumor architecture that promotes AR-pathway–independent subclones, which are selected or enriched under AR blockade and drive adaptive resistance. Clinically, tumors with high HTAV will exhibit poorer response to AR-targeted therapy (e.g., lower biochemical/radiographic responses and shorter time-to-progression) compared with low-HTAV tumors, independent of standard clinical covariates.
