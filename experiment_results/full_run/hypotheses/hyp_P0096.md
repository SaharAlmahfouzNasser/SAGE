# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

From the Ontologist synthesis, AR-targeted or chemo-primed tumors often show biomarker shifts (Decipher GC, PSA kinetics, IO predictive signatures) yet some patients fail to convert those signals into durable survival gains — i.e., why do biomarker-rich, therapy-primed tumors sometimes not achieve improved overall survival? 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on Core Path 1 (Decipher GC linking therapy to long-term outcomes) and Core Path 3 (IO predictive signatures as a therapy-modulated immune readout). These directly connect tumor-intrinsic genomic risk (Decipher) and tumor-immune readouts (IO signatures) to clinical outcomes after AR-directed or neoadjuvant therapy. 3) Tier 2 support: CENPA, Sox2/NE programs, and inflammation (EDIH) provide mechanistic routes by which tumor-intrinsic aggressiveness (chromosomal instability, lineage plasticity) or pro/anti-inflammatory states could disable effective immune organization; PSA trajectories offer an orthogonal, therapy-responsive kinetic readout that can help time when immune dysfunction emerges. 4) Why OS: Overall survival is the most definitive, clinically meaningful endpoint that integrates metastatic progression and treatment resistance — appropriate for testing whether biomarker discordance predicts failure to translate biomarker signals into durable benefit. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded exploratory/indirect paths (SPC, alpha-fetoprotein, neurocognitive uptake) from the core claim because they are distal to the tumor–immune–genomic axis and would dilute mechanistic focus. I limited the hypothesis to one imaging feature and two anchor molecular features to meet the Focusing Rule and keep the question testable and mechanistic?

## Working Hypothesis

In localized high-risk prostate cancer, the presence of TLS-like lymphoid aggregates indicates a potential anti-tumor immune microenvironment, but high intrinsic tumor aggressiveness captured by Decipher GC reverses or blunts TLS-driven anti-tumor activity by promoting immune suppression/exhaustion, leading IO predictive signatures to be non-predictive in TLS-positive, Decipher-high tumors. Consequently, TLS-positive tumors with high Decipher risk and discordant IO signatures will not exhibit improved OS versus TLS-negative tumors, indicating that high Decipher risk overrides beneficial TLS/IO signals and identifying patients unlikely to derive durable benefit from IO-modulated therapies.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
