# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlights that PARP inhibition (Olaparib) intersects tumor immune context and genomic classifiers, yet TLS- or TIL-rich microenvironments do not always produce durable antitumor responses or clinical benefit. The key puzzle is: why does an apparently immune-receptive TME (TLS/TIL-rich) sometimes fail to translate PARP-induced DNA damage into effective antitumor immunity and measurable therapy benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

Among TLS-like lymphoid aggregate–rich prostate tumors, high Decipher GC scores identify intrinsic transcriptional programs that impair neoantigen presentation and TLS function, blunting antitumor immunity elicited by PARP-inhibitor–induced DNA damage. Consequently, TLS-high tumors with high Decipher GC will exhibit poorer therapy_response to Olaparib than TLS-high/Decipher-low tumors, indicating a need for combinatorial immunomodulatory strategies.
