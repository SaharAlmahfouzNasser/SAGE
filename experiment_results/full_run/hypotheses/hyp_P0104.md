# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlighted that docetaxel-treated prostate cancers show divergent transcriptome remodeling and resistance phenotypes—why do some tumors become docetaxel-resistant despite exposure? 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on the MGMT mRNA path (path 1) because it offers a provocative, measurable resistance readout, and the full-length AR mRNA/AR path (path 2) because AR transcript integrity is a central, mechanistic determinant of prostate tumor state and therapy sensitivity. These two anchors directly suggest a cell-intrinsic repair/signaling axis that could enable survival under taxane pressure. 3) Tier 2 support: Evidence that docetaxel alters transcriptomes (transfrags from DU145 RNA-seq) and that epigenetic reprogramming (DZNep → KRT7 P1) can unmask alternative transcripts supports a model where microenvironmental or epigenetic states change transcript architecture and repair programs, reinforcing AR/MGMT-driven resistance. 4) Why therapy_response endpoint: The paths explicitly concern treatment (docetaxel) and resistance; a therapy_response proxy is the most direct, actionable clinical endpoint for testing mechanistic links between tumor state, transcripts, and drug outcome. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded the software/data-pipeline node (Oases) and any broad, multi-gene predictive lists to keep the hypothesis sharply mechanistic (max 2 anchor genes). SOX2, transfrags, KRT7 P1, DU145, and DZNep were placed in secondary_modifiers for follow-up rather than in the core claim to avoid diluting the testable question?

## Working Hypothesis

Do tumors with a high stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) resist docetaxel because a stromal-rich microenvironment preserves full-length AR mRNA and upregulates MGMT mRNA–driven stress-repair programs that enable survival under taxane pressure? If so, TSR-high tumors with elevated AR and MGMT mRNA expression will show poorer therapy response to docetaxel, identifying patients who may benefit from AR-directed or repair-targeted combination strategies.
