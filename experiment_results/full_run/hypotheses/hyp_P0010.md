## Working Hypothesis

Question: In muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), does a high stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) reflecting TGF-β–driven fibrotic stroma negate the immunogenic advantage conferred by MSH6 deficiency and elevated neoantigen load, by creating an immune-excluding microenvironment that impedes cytotoxic T-cell infiltration and activation? If this is true, then TSR-high tumors with TGFB1 activity and MSH6 deficiency will show poor therapy_response to checkpoint inhibitors, despite high neoantigen burden, implying that combining TGF-β–targeting or anti-fibrotic strategies with immunotherapy or selecting patients by TSR/TGFB1/MSH6 status could restore clinical benefit.
