# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Tier 1 synthesis highlights that EMT programs (vimentin) and TOP2β-related biology both associate with anthracycline exposure in TNBC, yet clinical responses vary widely — why do some EMT-high TNBC tumors fail to respond to anthracyclines despite TOP2β engagement?

## Working Hypothesis

Elevated stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) characterizes an ECM-rich microenvironment in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) that promotes an EMT-high tumor cell state (VIM-high) and modulates TOP2B engagement by anthracyclines. This triad—TSR-high ECM barriers, VIM-associated EMT signaling, and TOP2B-mediated DNA damage—reduces intratumoral anthracycline penetration and enhances DNA damage tolerance, leading to diminished therapy_response. Consequently, TNBC tumors with high TSR plus high VIM and robust TOP2B activity will exhibit poor response to anthracycline-based therapy. Disrupting ECM architecture or EMT signaling, or altering TOP2B engagement, will improve cytotoxic efficacy and restore sensitivity, particularly when combined with anthracyclines. The miR-223 axis provides a regulatory layer that can tune EMT/ECM programs and thereby modulate therapy_response. Primary predictive readout: TSR combined with tumor VIM and TOP2B status. Secondary modulators (MMP2/9, CK19, HSP70, ferritin, PD-L1, MMR) will provide supporting context and may refine stratification. Specific, testable predictions include: (a) high-TSR/VIM/TOP2B tumors show reduced doxorubicin efficacy in vitro and in vivo; (b) ECM-targeting or EMT-inhibitory interventions restore sensitivity; (c) miR-223 manipulations shift EMT/ECM signatures and alter drug response; (d) measurement of drug penetration correlates with TSR; (e) DDR and TOP2B activity markers track with response across models.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
