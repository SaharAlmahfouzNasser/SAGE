# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlights a diet–metabolism → inflammation axis (EDIP/EDIH → insulin/inflammatory markers) that interfaces with the tumor immune microenvironment; the key puzzle is why immune-infiltrated tumors (immune presence on histology) do not always yield improved prognosis — i.e., what systemic/metabolic conditions blunt the protective effect of intratumoral immune infiltration. 2) Tier 1 paths used and why: I built on the EDIH → inflammation/insulin-response path (Tier 1) because it directly links systemic hyperinsulinemic/dietary exposure to measurable insulin-response markers that can plausibly reprogram tumor and myeloid metabolism; I also used the imaging/immune readout axis (choose TIL density as the available H&E-derived immune imaging feature) to represent the immune state captured noninvasively. These two axes together let us ask why immune presence sometimes fails to confer benefit. 3) Tier 2 evidence supporting the claim: Tier 2 paths supply mechanistic modifiers that could mediate this interaction — altered tumor polyamine metabolism (spermine) and chromosomal instability/aggressiveness markers (CENPA) can alter tumor immunogenicity; the OPN → CSF-1 macrophage axis (and SPC readout) gives a concrete mechanism for insulin-driven recruitment/activation of immunosuppressive myeloid cells that would impair T-cell effector function. 4) Why OS as endpoint: Overall survival is the most robust, hard clinical endpoint available in TCGA-BRCA and is best suited to capture the net, long-term consequence of immune–metabolic interactions on patient outcome. 5) What I excluded deliberately and why: I excluded multi-feature models (kept anchors to only EDIH + insulin-response markers) and omitted imaging radiomics/composite biomarkers and EDIP from the core hypothesis to keep the question sharply mechanistic and testable; other exploratory/cross-cancer paths (AFP, neuroimaging endpoints, assay-specific SPC details) were relegated to secondary modifiers or ignored to avoid overfitting the core mechanistic claim?

## Working Hypothesis

In breast cancer, the prognostic benefit of high tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) density on overall survival is conditional on systemic insulin exposure. Specifically, TCGA-BRCA patients with high TIL density and low insulin exposure (low EDIH index and low insulin-response gene signature activity) will exhibit improved OS, whereas high insulin exposure (high EDIH and/or elevated insulin-response signature) will attenuate or negate the TIL-associated OS benefit through insulin-driven immunometabolic reprogramming that promotes pro-tumor immunosuppressive myeloid programs (e.g., CSF1 signaling and osteopontin pathways) and impairs T-cell effector function. This interaction should appear as a significant TIL density × insulin exposure interaction in Cox models for OS and be supported by correlations with myeloid/immune microenvironment markers (CSF1, OPN, M2 macrophage/ exhaustion signatures). Secondary modifiers (e.g., spermine, CENPA) may modulate effect size but are not required for the primary test. The hypothesis will be evaluated in TCGA-BRCA with imaging-derived TIL density, insulin-related anchors (EDIh and insulin-response gene signature proxies), and immune/metabolic pathway readouts; findings will be validated in independent datasets and, where feasible, complemented by mechanistic in vitro and in vivo experiments.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
