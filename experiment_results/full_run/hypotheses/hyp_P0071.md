# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Stromal TIL density correlates with an active immune milieu yet not all TIL-high TNBC achieve pCR after NACT — why does morphological infiltration sometimes fail to translate into effective chemo-cooperative anti-tumor immunity? 2) Tier 1 path used: the single path linking stromal TIL density → IFN-γ–related transcriptional programs → pCR; I built on this because IFNG is the canonical driver of cytotoxic/chemokine programs and HLA-DRA represents tumor/antigen-presentation competence required for effector function. 3) Tier 2 supporting evidence (used to justify mechanisms but not in the core claim): downstream effectors and modulators such as CXCL9/CXCL10 (T cell recruitment), GZMB/PRF1 (cytotoxic effectors), STAT1 (IFN signaling mediator) and IDO1 (immunosuppressive counter-regulator) provide a mechanistic rationale for how IFNG/HLA-DRA status would modulate functional immunity and response to chemo. 4) Endpoint choice: therapy_response (pCR) is the most relevant, measurable neoadjuvant outcome in TNBC and directly tests the proposed immune–chemo cooperation mechanism. 5) What I excluded and why: I limited the core claim to exactly one imaging feature (TIL density) plus two anchor genes (IFNG, HLA-DRA) to keep the hypothesis sharply mechanistic; I excluded TLS as a core variable to avoid conflating two morphological features and relegated additional immune genes (CXCL9, CXCL10, GZMB, PRF1, STAT1, IDO1) to secondary_modifiers for follow-up interrogation?

## Working Hypothesis

Are TIL-high TNBCs that fail to achieve pCR after neoadjuvant chemotherapy characterized by deficient IFN-γ signaling and/or impaired HLA-DRA–mediated antigen presentation, rendering infiltrating T cells functionally inert? If so, integrating stromal TIL density with IFNG and HLA-DRA expression (and related IFN-signaling modifiers such as CXCL9, CXCL10, GZMB, PRF1, STAT1, and IDO1) will improve pCR prediction beyond morphology alone.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
