# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

Do breast tumors with a high stromal-to-tumor ratio and harboring TP53 mutations with reduced CDH1 expression undergo EMT-driven invasion that mechanistically links loss of cell–cell adhesion to pro-invasive stromal remodeling, leading to accelerated local progression? If this is true, tumors with the TP53 mutation and low CDH1 in a high-TSR context will have shorter progression-free intervals in TCGA-BRCA compared with tumors lacking one or more of these features, identifying a biologically coherent subset at high risk of early progression.


| Element | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Imaging feature | Stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) |
| Anchor gene(s) | TP53, CDH1 |
| Primary endpoint | PFI |


# 2. Why This Gene — Not Others

## Primary hypothesis gene(s)

**TP53:** TP53 is frequently mutated in breast, both consistent with a conserved TP53–CDH1 axis modulating invasiveness; stromal remodeling (reflected by stromal-to-tumor ratio) is a plausible imaging correlate of EMT-driven desmoplasia.

**CDH1:** CDH1 axis modulating invasiveness; stromal remodeling (reflected by stromal-to-tumor ratio) is a plausible imaging correlate of EMT-driven desmoplasia.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Analyze TCGA-BRCA and independent datasets to quantify TSR from histology, determine TP53 mutation status, and measure CDH1 expression;

**Prediction 2:** assess EMT activity via gene signatures and key protein markers;

**Prediction 3:** test for a three-way interaction (TSR x TP53 status x CDH1 expression) on progression-free interval using Cox models, with replication in external cohorts;


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.

## Caveats & Risks

- Gene expression does not equal protein activity; phospho-protein or functional assays are needed for confirmation.

## Alternative Directions

- Klebsiella: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
- EMT: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
