# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Why do some high-risk localized prostate cancers with abundant lymphocyte infiltration nonetheless fail to respond to intensified systemic therapies? The Ontologist synthesis highlights a triad—tumor-intrinsic genomic risk (Decipher GC), the functional immune state (IO predictive signatures), and systemic inflammation/metabolic context (EDIH/insulin signals)—that could explain this paradox?

## Working Hypothesis

Among men with high-risk localized prostate cancer, the predictive value of tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs) for benefit from intensified systemic therapy is context-dependent. We hypothesize that: (1) Decipher high-risk tumors with abundant TILs and an exhausted immune phenotype (IO exhaustion signature) will exhibit reduced response to intensified neoadjuvant/systemic therapy due to T cell dysfunction and supportive myeloid/immunometabolic remodeling; (2) Decipher low-risk tumors with high TILs and an active effector IO signature will show greater therapeutic benefit from intensified regimens; (3) Secondary modifiers (CSF-1–driven macrophage remodeling, PD-L1 checkpoint signaling, PTGER4 signaling, CENPA-associated proliferative drive, EDIH/metabolic/inflammatory signaling, and insulin_response_markers) will modulate the magnitude of the TIL–Decipher–IO interaction by either dampening or enhancing T cell function and therapy responsiveness. Accordingly, the primary endpoint (therapy_response) will be driven by a three-way interaction: TIL density × Decipher GC status × IO exhaustion state, with secondary modifiers shaping the strength and direction of this interaction. This framework predicts: a) TIL-high/Decipher-high with IO exhaustion will have the lowest therapy_response rates; b) TIL-high/Decipher-low with IO effector signatures will have the highest response rates; and c) Tier-2 modifiers will attenuate or amplify these effects in a dose-dependent manner.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
