# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Tier 1 analysis raises the question of why PI3K-targeted/metabolic therapies and genomic classifiers (PIK3CA status; proliferation-based RS) give discordant predictions of benefit — specifically, why some PIK3CA-mutant HR+/HER2– tumors do not show clinical response to PI3Kα inhibition?

## Working Hypothesis

Therapy_response to PI3Kα inhibition in HR+/HER2− breast cancer is governed by a three-way interaction among tumor intrinsic PI3K activation (PIK3CA mutation), proliferation status (MKI67), and stromal content (stroma-to-tumor ratio, TSR). Specifically, PIK3CA-mutant, MKI67-high, TSR-low tumors will exhibit reduced clinical response to alpelisib (± metformin) due to tumor-cell–centric metabolic rewiring with insufficient stromal buffering to dissipate PI3K blockade–induced metabolic stress. Conversely, PIK3CA-mutant tumors with MKI67-low or TSR-high, and non-mutant tumors with favorable microenvironmental context, will show greater responsiveness. This interaction should be detectable in correlative analyses of clinical trial samples (pre-treatment MKI67 IHC, PIK3CA sequencing, TSR from pathology or radiomics, and imaging/metabolic biomarkers) and mechanistically validated in preclinical systems by modulating proliferation and stromal context in tumor organoid/co-culture models treated with PI3Kα inhibitors (with/without metformin) and assessing viability, metabolic stress markers, and compensatory pathway activation.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** correlative analyses within PI3Kα inhibitor trials assessing PIK3CA status, MKI67, TSR, and imaging/metabolic biomarkers against therapy_response;

**Prediction 2:** preclinical organoid/fibroblast co-cultures to manipulate TSR and proliferation and measure viability and metabolic stress under PI3Kα inhibition ± metformin;

**Prediction 3:** mechanistic readouts such as metabolic flux, lactate production, and activation of compensatory pathways (e.g., mTORC1, AMPK).


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions
