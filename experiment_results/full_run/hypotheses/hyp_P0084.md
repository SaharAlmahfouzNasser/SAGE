# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In BRCA2-deficient breast tumors, therapy_response to PARP inhibition (e.g., Olaparib) is determined by the pre-treatment intratumoral immune context: high baseline TIL density and elevated CD8A-expressing cytotoxic T cells enable PARP inhibitor–induced DNA damage to generate neoantigens that are effectively presented (via HLA-DQB1) and targeted by CD8+ T cells, producing durable anti-tumor immunity. BRCA2-deficient tumors with low TIL density or low CD8A expression will exhibit poor therapy_response despite genomic susceptibility. PARP1 serves as a secondary anchor that modulates the magnitude of DNA damage signaling, potentially amplifying or dampening the immune readout. Tier 2 modifiers (HLA-DQB1, CSF1, IL6, CRP, osteopontin) influence antigen presentation and the immunosuppressive microenvironment, further shaping the likelihood and durability of therapy_response. The testable predictions are: (1) among BRCA2-deficient breast cancers, those with high TIL density and high CD8A will show higher therapy_response to Olaparib; (2) modulators of antigen presentation and macrophage polarization (e.g., HLA-DQB1, CSF1) and systemic inflammation markers (IL6, CRP) will stratify responders from non-responders within this BRCA2-deficient cohort.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** retrospective multi-omics analysis in BRCA2-deficient breast cancer cohorts with documented PARP inhibitor response (assessing TIL density, CD8A, HLA-DQB1, CSF1, IL6, CRP);

**Prediction 2:** in vitro co-culture systems of BRCA2-deficient breast cancer cells with CD8+ T cells ± PARP inhibitor to measure antigen presentation and cytotoxicity;

**Prediction 3:** in vivo BRCA2-deficient mouse models to test PARP inhibition with and without modulation of CSF1/IL6 pathways and to evaluate TIL recruitment and therapy_response. This approach provides a mechanistic, testable framework with clear clinical relevance for selecting BRCA2-mutant patients for PARP inhibitor therapy.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
