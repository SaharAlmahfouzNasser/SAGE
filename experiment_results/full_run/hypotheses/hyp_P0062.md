# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Why do some tumors with favorable immune architecture still fail to achieve better clinical outcomes?

## Working Hypothesis

In triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) treated with neoadjuvant chemotherapy, the predictive value of stromal TIL density (sTIL%) for pathologic complete response (pCR) is contingent on spatial organization: high sTIL% predicts higher pCR only when TILs are enriched in close proximity to tumor nests or at the tumor–stroma border. To test this, we hypothesize that (1) a composite morphological score that combines sTIL% with a proximity/organization index (e.g., fraction of TILs within defined radii of tumor nests, mean distance from TILs to tumor nests, and a TIL clustering metric) will outperform sTIL% alone in predicting pCR; (2) there will be a significant interaction between sTIL% and the proximity/organization index, such that the strongest association with pCR occurs when both high density and favorable spatial arrangement are present; (3) this morphology-based predictor will maintain predictive power across tumor heterogeneity and treatment regimens and can be implemented from standard H&E slides. Experimental testing will involve retrospective discovery and external validation cohorts, with digital pathology pipelines to quantify sTIL% and spatial metrics, followed by multivariable modeling (including interaction terms) and assessment of discriminative performance (AUC, calibration).

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
