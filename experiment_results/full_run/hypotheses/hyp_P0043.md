# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In HER2-positive breast cancer, a high stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) establishes an ECM-rich, angiogenic niche that elevates serum VEGF and promotes tumor cell intravasation, reflected by an unfavorable CTC response during HER2-targeted therapy. Consequently, tumors with high TSR, elevated VEGF, and poor CTC clearance will exhibit diminished therapy_response, identifying a subset that may benefit from adjunctive anti-angiogenic or stromal-targeting interventions.


| Element | Value |

| Imaging feature | Stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) |
| Anchor gene(s) | serum VEGF concentration, CTC response |

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Clinical correlative study: quantify TSR in diagnostic/post-treatment tumor sections by histology, measure baseline and on-treatment serum VEGF by ELISA, monitor CTC burden/kinetics using established platforms (e.g., CellSearch or microfluidic capture), and correlate with objective therapy_response (pCR or radiologic/biochemical response) in HER2+ cohorts.

**Prediction 2:** Preclinical validation: employ HER2+ breast cancer models with manipulated stromal content (e.g., co-culture with fibroblasts or ECM-modulating enzymes; or patient-derived xenografts with variable ECM density) to measure VEGF secretion, CTC surrogates, and response to HER2 inhibitors alone and with anti-angiogenic/stroma-targeting agents.

**Prediction 3:** Mechanistic experiments: assess whether TSR- or VEGF-driven changes in vascular permeability and ECM remodeling mediate CTC extravasation using in vivo imaging (DCE-MRI, IVIS for labeled cells) and ECM remodeling assays.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.

## Caveats & Risks

- Hypothesis is computationally derived and requires experimental validation.

## Alternative Directions

- Breast Cancer Index (BCI): Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
- 70-gene assay: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
