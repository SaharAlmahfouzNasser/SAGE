# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

Primary hypothesis: In HER2-positive breast cancer, elevated MKI67 mRNA expression (RNA-seq_MKI67_signal) is independently associated with increased tumor cell proliferation and shorter overall survival after adjusting for standard clinicopathologic covariates; this prognostic association is modified by trastuzumab therapy such that the strength and direction of the MKI67–OS relationship differ by trastuzumab exposure. Secondary hypotheses: (1) MKI67 mRNA expression correlates with the PAM50 proliferation score and with lower immune infiltration, and a combined MKI67 + PAM50 proliferation score provides superior prognostic discrimination versus either marker alone; (2) The MKI67–OS association is reproducible in external HER2-positive cohorts and correlates with IHC Ki-67 on matched samples, supporting clinical transferability; (3) In preclinical models, modulation of MKI67 expression alters proliferation and impacts sensitivity to HER2-targeted therapies, suggesting a functional role in therapy response and potential as a therapeutic biomarker.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
