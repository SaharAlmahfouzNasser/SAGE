# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), tumors that are TLS-rich by histology but harbor very high TMB and deficient mismatch repair (dMMR) exhibit dysfunctional TLS maturation and CD8+ T-cell exhaustion driven by extensive neoantigen heterogeneity, leading to suboptimal response to immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) compared with TLS-rich tumors with lower heterogeneity or TLS-poor tumors with similar TMB/dMMR. Therefore, TLS presence alone is insufficient as a predictor of ICI response in the TMB-high/dMMR setting; integrated biomarkers that combine (a) TLS maturation status (e.g., presence of germinal centers, organized B/T compartments), (b) clonal vs subclonal neoantigen burden (neoantigen clonality/heterogeneity), and (c) MMR/TMB status will better stratify patients for immunotherapy. This model yields testable predictions: (1) TLS maturation scores will correlate with ICI responses primarily in tumors with high clonal neoantigen burden; (2) among TLS-rich, TMB-high/dMMR tumors, those with a high fraction of subclonal neoantigens will show reduced objective response rates and shorter progression-free survival due to TLS dysfunction and T-cell exhaustion; (3) patients with discordant TLS maturation and neoantigen clonality will benefit from combination strategies targeting TLS restoration or T-cell exhaustion pathways. Secondary modifiers (TP53, DPYD, GARS1, LRPPRC) may modulate these relationships by influencing tumor biology, metabolism, and immune contexture, but the primary predictive signal arises from the interaction of TLS architecture, TMB/dMMR, and neoantigen clonality.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
