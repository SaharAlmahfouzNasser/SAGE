# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Tier 1 synthesis identifies a recurring tension — imaging/pathology markers of organized local immunity (in-situ infiltrates, e.g., TLS) should confer survival benefit, yet that benefit is inconsistent across patients; systemic inflammatory programs (EDIH/EDIP) and specific inflammatory signaling nodes (TNFα-R2) may convert organized immune structures into dysfunctional or immunosuppressive niches. 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on the two in-situ immune infiltrate paths (enzalutamide and abiraterone → pathomics/radiomics → infiltrate status) because they directly link measurable tissue organization to outcome, and on the abiraterone → EDIH → inflammation biomarkers path because it provides a mechanistic bridge between systemic inflammation and local immune state; TNFα-R2 emerges as a plausible molecular effector in those inflammatory circuits. 3) Tier 2 support: EDIP/insulin-response and radiomics/pathomics readouts reinforce a coupling between metabolic-inflammatory state and imaging-derived immune surrogates, suggesting measurable systemic modifiers (EDIP/insulin markers) and orthogonal image-based validators (radiomics/pathomics). 4) Why OS: Overall survival is the most robust, clinically meaningful endpoint available in the ontologist’s synthesis (used in the nomogram paths) and best captures the net impact of immune/inflammatory interactions on patient prognosis. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded SPC, spermine, PSA-specific dynamics and other exploratory metabolic nodes (Tier 3) to keep the hypothesis sharply focused on the immune-organization vs. inflammatory signaling puzzle; additional genes/entities (EDIP, radiomics/pathomics, insulin-response markers) are placed as secondary_modifiers for follow-up testing?

## Working Hypothesis

In metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC) patients treated with androgen receptor pathway inhibitors, TLS-like lymphoid aggregates within the tumor microenvironment predict improved overall survival only if local inflammatory signaling via TNFα receptor 2 (TNFR2) is low and systemic inflammatory burden is low (as indexed by a low energy-adjusted Dietary Inflammatory Index, EDIH). When TNFR2 signaling is high and/or the systemic inflammatory state is elevated (high EDIH/EDIP and insulin-resistance markers), TLS-rich tumors will fail to confer survival benefit, instead forming immunosuppressive niches characterized by reduced CD8+ T cell density and effector function, impaired dendritic cell maturation, and skewed myeloid phenotypes. This switch is driven by TNFR2+ regulatory T cells and TNFR2-expressing stromal/endothelial cells within TLS, amplifying suppression of anti-tumor immunity. Consequently, TLS-rich tumors with high TNFR2 and high EDIH will have worse or equivalent OS compared with TLS-poor tumors, whereas TLS-rich tumors with low TNFR2 and low EDIH will show superior OS. Radiomics and pathomics features will correlate with TLS maturation state and inflammatory signaling, serving as noninvasive surrogates for this immunobiology. Clinically, this implies a stratification axis where TLS presence alone is insufficient; the prognostic value of TLS depends on TNFR2 activity and systemic dietary-inflammatory state, identifying patients who may benefit from combined TNFR2-targeted or anti-inflammatory interventions (e.g., dietary modification, metabolic/inflammatory therapy) to realize TLS-associated survival gains.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** At the tissue level, tumors with the imaging feature but low anchor gene activity will show immune cells confined to structural peripheries rather than infiltrating tumor nests.

**Prediction 2:** In functional experiments, restoring or enhancing the anchor gene's signaling will improve immune-mediated tumor control in imaging-feature-positive models.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
