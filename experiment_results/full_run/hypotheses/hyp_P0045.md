# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlighted that organized intratumoral immune structures and canonical immune biomarkers (PD-L1, MMR) do not consistently predict clinical benefit in HER2+ breast cancer — i.e., why do some TLS- or TIL-rich tumors still fail to achieve improved outcomes? 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built primarily on the PD-L1/MMR cross-cancer immunobiology path (Path 1) and the THR cell-of-origin signature path (Path 3). PD-L1 links immune checkpoint biology to tumor immune engagement, while the THR origin signature provides a cell-intrinsic transcriptional program that can modulate antigen presentation, immune recruitment, and the functional quality of lymphoid aggregates. 3) Tier 2 evidence supporting the claim: Recurrence Score (RS) and BRENDA score indicate that intrinsic subtype/proliferation context modifies prognostic impact of immune features; DNA methylation and non-targeted proteomics suggest that epigenetic/proteomic states can suppress or enable antigen-presentation and checkpoint expression; these support a model where lineage/program-level features gate whether TLS confer benefit. 4) Why OS as endpoint: Overall survival is the most definitive, robust endpoint in TCGA-BRCA for assessing whether an immune/lineage interaction translates into durable patient benefit across therapies; this question asks why immune imaging features sometimes fail to improve OS. 5) What I excluded and why: I limited core anchors to PD-L1 and the THR cell-of-origin signature to keep the mechanistic claim sharp; I intentionally excluded TLR4 and MMR proteins from the two-anchor core (they are placed as secondary modifiers) to avoid overcomplicating the primary causal statement. Cardiac biomarkers (CK-MB) and Tier-3 metabolic markers (Apo B, BMI) were excluded from the hypothesis because they do not address the central immune–lineage mechanistic puzzle?

## Working Hypothesis

Mechanistic claim: In HER2-positive breast cancer, the presence of TLS-like lymphoid aggregates translates into an overall survival advantage only when the tumor harbors a THR cell-of-origin signature and high PD-L1 expression; in THR-high, PD-L1-high tumors, TLS provide a microenvironment that enhances antigen presentation and CD8+ T cell activity, whereas THR-low or PD-L1-low tumors with TLS fail to convert organized immune structure into durable anti-tumor immunity due to impaired antigen presentation and potential T cell exhaustion. Clinical corollary: TLS presence will be associated with improved OS specifically in PD-L1-high, THR-signature-positive HER2+ tumors; this association will be attenuated or reversed in TLS-positive, THR-negative tumors. Predictive value and modifiers: The magnitude of the TLS effect will be modulated by secondary modifiers such as MMR status, Recurrence Score, BRENDA score, and DNA methylation/epigenetic states, accounting for heterogeneity across patients. Testing plan (summary): 1) Retrospective cohort analyses to evaluate three-way interactions TLS presence × PD-L1 status × THR signature on OS, PFI, and therapy response proxies; 2) validation in independent datasets; 3) mechanistic in vitro assays to show THR-driven changes in antigen presentation (MHC I/II) and PD-L1 induction under IFN-γ signaling; 4) preclinical models to test whether THR manipulation alters TLS efficacy and survival outcomes; 5) integration of imaging, transcriptomic, and epigenetic data to refine a triad biomarker for patient stratification.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
