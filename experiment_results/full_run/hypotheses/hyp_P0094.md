# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

Primary hypothesis: In localized prostate cancer treated with curative intent, cribriform architectural fraction (CAF) interacts with CCP score to determine progression-free interval (PFI). Specifically, CAF-high tumors with high CCP scores will have the shortest PFI, while CAF-high/CCP-low tumors will have intermediate PFI, and CAF-low tumors (regardless of CCP) will have the longest PFI. Mechanistically, CAF reflects architecture-associated proliferative programs; when these programs are amplified (high CCP), the proliferative drive accelerates progression. Testable predictions: (1) A statistically significant CAF*CCP interaction term in a Cox model predicting PFI after adjusting for Gleason grade, preoperative PSA, PTEN loss, and ERG status. (2) Stratified analyses show CAF-high/CCP-high has the worst PFI, followed by CAF-high/CCP-low, with CAF-low groups faring best. (3) The interaction remains significant after adjusting for known clinical covariates; (4) PTEN loss or ERG status may modulate the interaction magnitude as secondary modifiers. Clinically, this supports risk-stratified approaches for adjuvant therapy in CAF-rich tumors with high proliferative signals.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.

## Caveats & Risks

- Hypothesis is computationally derived and requires experimental validation.

## Alternative Directions

- Gleason grade: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
- Preoperative PSA: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
