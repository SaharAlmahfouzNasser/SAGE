# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

TLS-like lymphoid aggregates in localized prostate tumors are not consistently associated with improved therapy response when tumors co-express high PTGER4 and high PD-L1. The PGE2–PTGER4 signaling axis amplifies PD-L1–driven T-cell exhaustion and impairs TLS maturation, preventing functional TLS-mediated anti-tumor activity. Specifically, PTGER4hi/PD-L1hi TLS-rich tumors will exhibit reduced objective responses to standard therapies (including PD-(L)1 inhibitors) due to (a) maintenance of exhausted CD8+ T cells (elevated PD-1, TIM-3, LAG-3, TOX; diminished IFN-γ, granzyme B), (b) impaired germinal center–like maturation within TLS (lower CXCL13, BCL6+ B cells, follicular dendritic cell networks), and (c) a suppressive cytokine milieu that sustains a non-responsive microenvironment. Consequently, blockade of PTGER4 signaling or PGE2 synthesis in combination with PD-(L)1 pathway inhibition will restore TLS maturation and T-cell functionality, improving treatment efficacy. Systemic inflammatory status (CRP, IL-6), dietary inflammatory indices (EDIH/EDIP), and metabolic signals (insulin/metabolite profiles) will modulate the PTGER4–PD-L1 axis and related imaging phenotypes, acting as contextual modifiers of response and helping to identify patients most likely to benefit from combination strategies. Predictions: (i) TLS-rich, PTGER4hi/PD-L1hi tumors show poorer response to monotherapy compared with PTGER4lo/PD-L1lo TLS-rich tumors; (ii) PTGER4 antagonism or PGE2 synthesis inhibition reshapes TLS architecture toward maturation and increases functional T-cell readouts; (iii) combined PTGER4/PGE2 blockade with PD-(L)1 blockade yields higher response rates in TLS-rich contexts; (iv) radiomic/pathomic features will correlate with PTGER4/PD-L1 status and predict response when integrated with systemic inflammatory/metabolic indices.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
