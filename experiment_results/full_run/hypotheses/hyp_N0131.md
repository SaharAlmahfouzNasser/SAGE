# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

Do TLS-like lymphoid aggregates in BRCA1-deficient breast tumors enable PARP1 inhibition–induced DNA damage from talazoparib to be converted into productive anti-tumor immunity, thereby improving therapy_response to the talazoparib–pembrolizumab combination? If so, TLS-high BRCA1-deficient tumors will show higher objective therapy_response to the combination than TLS-low tumors, whereas TLS-low tumors will have inferior responses that may require additional PD-1 axis modulation to achieve comparable outcomes.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** In vitro BRCA1-deficient vs. BRCA1-proficient models treated with talazoparib to measure DNA damage (γH2AX), cytosolic DNA, type I IFN signaling, and upregulation of antigen presentation machinery (MHC I/II).

**Prediction 2:** In vivo BRCA1-deficient syngeneic breast cancer models with TLS quantification by multiplex immunohistochemistry to distinguish TLS-high vs TLS-low tumors; genetic or pharmacologic modulation of TLS formation may be used to validate causality.

**Prediction 3:** Treatment arms with talazoparib, pembrolizumab, or their combination; endpoints include therapy_response, tumor growth, survival, and immune correlates (CD8+ T cell infiltration, TCR clonality, IFN-st Signature).


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
