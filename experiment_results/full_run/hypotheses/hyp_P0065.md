# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

TNBC tumors often present with dense lymphocytic infiltrates, yet high TIL density does not uniformly translate into improved survival; the Ontologist's tiers point to a competing immunosuppressive TAM axis (CD163) and tumor-derived circulating miRNA (miR-6855-5p) as candidate modulators that could blunt TIL efficacy. 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on the CD163/TAM path because it directly explains local immune suppression, and the miR-6855-5p/EV path because secreted miRNAs provide a plausible tumor-to-immune-cell reprogramming mechanism; I referenced the HbA1c/metabolic path for context but did not include it as a core anchor to preserve focus. 3) Tier 2 evidence supporting the claim: Hedgehog/CCND1 and proliferation programs can increase tumor intrinsic resistance to immune control (providing a proliferative competitor to immune elimination); LINE‑1 methylation and systemic metabolic markers (HbA1c) support a metabolic/epigenetic milieu that may cooperate with TAMs to impair anti-tumor immunity. 4) Endpoint choice: Overall survival (OS) was chosen because it is the most robust, well-annotated clinical endpoint in TCGA-BRCA and aligns with the Ontologist’s path linking miRNAs to OS. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded TLS-like aggregates and TSR from the core hypothesis to avoid conflating distinct immune-architectural readouts; I relegated systemic metabolic marker HbA1c and pathway nodes (CCND1, Hedgehog, LINE-1) to secondary_modifiers to keep the mechanistic question sharp and testable with one imaging feature and up to two molecular anchors?

## Working Hypothesis

In TNBC, high TIL density alone does not guarantee improved overall survival unless the tumor microenvironment lacks a dominant TAM-immunosuppressive axis and tumor-derived extracellular miRNA cargo. Specifically, TNBC tumors that are TIL-high but also CD163-high and miR-6855-5p-high will show worse OS than TIL-high, CD163-low, miR-6855-5p-low tumors. Mechanistically, CD163+ tumor-associated macrophages secrete immunosuppressive cytokines (eg IL-10, TGF-β) and create a milieu that dampens T cell cytotoxicity and antigen presentation. Concurrently, tumor-derived EVs carrying miR-6855-5p are taken up by CD8+ T cells and dendritic cells, downregulating components of IFN-γ signaling, MHC class II and co-stimulatory pathways, and cytotoxic effector programs. The net result is a decoupling of TIL presence from effective anti-tumor immunity in the subset with high CD163 and high miR-6855-5p, explaining inferior OS despite abundant TILs. Testable predictions: (i) in TNBC cohorts, the interaction effect of TIL density with CD163 expression and miR-6855-5p EV levels better predicts OS than TIL density alone; (ii) tumors with high CD163 exhibit T cell and antigen presentation gene signatures that are suppressed relative to CD163-low tumors; (iii) in vitro, EVs enriched for miR-6855-5p suppress T cell cytotoxicity and DC antigen-presentation markers, reversible by anti-miR-6855-5p inhibition; (iv) in preclinical models, targeting TAMs or inhibiting miR-6855-5p function restores TIL activity and improves survival.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** retrospective cohort analyses with multivariate Cox models testing interactions among TIL density, CD163, and miR-6855-5p EV burden on OS;

**Prediction 2:** single-cell or bulk transcriptomics to compare immune signatures across CD163 strata;

**Prediction 3:** isolation and characterization of tumor-derived EVs, cargo profiling, and in vitro assays with human T cells/DCs to assess cytotoxicity, antigen presentation, and cytokine production;


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
