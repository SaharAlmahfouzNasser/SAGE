# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

High tumor budding density at the invasive front (BD-IF) is an independent predictor of shorter progression-free interval (PFI) in non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC), after adjusting for standard clinicopathologic factors (tumor grade, stage) and tumor-stroma context (TSR). BD-IF reflects an EMT-like local invasion phenotype that facilitates microinvasion and seeding of urothelial tissue, thereby increasing the risk of local progression and recurrence. The BD-IF metric, when quantified using standardized digital pathology (with predefined field selection, budding definitions, and automated/semi-automated counting) across centers, will improve risk stratification beyond existing models (e.g., risk tables based on grade/stage) and will yield meaningful reclassification for intensified surveillance or adjuvant therapy choices. The hypothesis will be tested by: (1) assembling retrospective multi-center NMIBC cohorts with long-term follow-up; (2) applying a pre-specified BD-IF scoring protocol, ideally with automated image analysis to measure bud density per invasive-front length or per high-power field; (3) evaluating BD-IF as an independent variable in multivariable Cox models for PFI, adjusting for grade, stage, TSR, age, and other covariates; (4) assessing incremental prognostic value using time-dependent AUC, C-index, NRI, and IDI; (5) validating findings in an external cohort; and (6) exploring correlations with EMT-associated morphological features as supportive, non-mandatory evidence. The approach aims for a robust, implementable biomarker that can be integrated into routine pathology reporting and clinical decision-making.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
