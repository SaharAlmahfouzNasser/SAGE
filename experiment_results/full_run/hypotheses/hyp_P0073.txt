1) Gland-centric architectural features
- Gland density: number of glands per square millimeter within the tumor region.

4) Spatial heterogeneity of glands
- Gland clustering tendency: graph-based metric (e.g., clustering coefficient) computed on a graph of gland centroids.
- Moran’s I or similar for spatial autocorrelation of gland density across the ROI (captures regional heterogeneity).
