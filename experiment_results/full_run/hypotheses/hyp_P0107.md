# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC) treated with docetaxel, a high stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) in pretreatment tumors is associated with reduced therapy_response. This association is mediated by two linked mechanisms: (1) TSR-driven microenvironmental cues promote AR signaling reactivation in tumor cells, and (2) AR signaling upregulates the drug-efflux transporter ABCB1, decreasing intratumoral docetaxel exposure and cytotoxicity. Consequently, TSR-high tumors with evidence of AR reactivation and elevated ABCB1 will exhibit the poorest therapy_response compared with TSR-low tumors. Secondary modifiers (CYP17A1, TUBB3, PTEN, SLCO2B1) may modulate the magnitude of these effects but are not required for the primary mechanism. The primary measurable endpoint to test this hypothesis is therapy_response (objective radiographic/biochemical response to docetaxel).

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Clinical correlative study measuring pretreatment TSR in tumor biopsies and associating with documented docetaxel therapy_response;

**Prediction 2:** longitudinal assessment of AR target gene expression and ABCB1 levels in tumors before/after docetaxel exposure;

**Prediction 3:** preclinical models (mCRPC cell lines, patient-derived xenografts) engineered or selected for TSR levels to test docetaxel accumulation and cytotoxicity, with AR antagonists or ABCB1 inhibitors to assess causality;


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
