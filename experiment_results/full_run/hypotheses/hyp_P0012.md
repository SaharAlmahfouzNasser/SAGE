# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Why do some MIBC tumors with robust local immune architecture (TLS/T-cell infiltration) nevertheless fail to benefit from immunotherapy? The Ontologist framed this as an interaction between a fibrogenic, TGF-β–dominated stroma and spatial immune organization (Immunoscore/TLS), with implications for immunotherapy response?

## Working Hypothesis

Do TLS-like lymphoid aggregates in MIBC fail to confer benefit from immune checkpoint blockade when tumor TGFB1 expression is high, driving stromal fibrosis and functional exclusion of TLS leading to an ineffective Immunoscore? If true, TLS-rich/TGFB1-high tumors will exhibit reduced response to PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors, indicating that combining TGF-β pathway inhibition with immunotherapy is required to restore TLS functionality and improve clinical outcomes.

| --- | --- |
| Imaging feature | TLS-like lymphoid aggregates |
| Anchor gene(s) | TGFB1, Immunoscore |

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Retrospective analysis of MIBC cohorts treated with PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors to assess interactions between TGFB1 expression, markers of fibrosis (e.g., COL1A1, FN1), TLS presence/organization, Immunoscore, and objective response rate (ORR)/PFS.

**Prediction 2:** Spatial profiling (IHC, multiplex immunofluorescence, spatial transcriptomics) to quantify TLS maturation, T cell exclusion gradients, and ECM deposition in TGFB1-high vs TGFB1-low tumors.

**Prediction 3:** Preclinical modeling (mouse or organoid systems) to test whether TGFB1 overexpression drives fibrosis and TLS dysfunction and whether TGF-β blockade restores TLS function and enhances responsiveness to anti-PD-1/PD-L1.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.

## Caveats & Risks
