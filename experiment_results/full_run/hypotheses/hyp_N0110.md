# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In tumors with RASSF1A loss, increased genomic instability and neoantigen burden recruit tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes but concurrently upregulate LAG3-mediated signaling, driving T cell exhaustion and compromising anti-tumor immunity; consequently, high TIL density will not translate into improved overall survival in RASSF1A-deficient tumors with elevated LAG3, when compared with RASSF1A-wildtype tumors, suggesting that these tumors may benefit from LAG3-targeted checkpoint blockade to restore TIL effectiveness.


| Element | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Imaging feature | Tumor-Infiltrating Lymphocyte (TIL) density |
| Anchor gene(s) | RASSF1A, LAG3 |
| Primary endpoint | OS |


# 2. Why This Gene — Not Others

## Primary hypothesis gene(s)

**RASSF1A:** RASSF1A (a tumor suppressor frequently silenced by promoter methylation) to immune context via LAG3: RASSF1A loss plausibly drives tumor programs (genomic instability, altered signaling/metabolism) that promote T cell dysfunction or checkpoint upregulation, with LAG3 as a candidate mediator.

**LAG3:** LAG3: RASSF1A loss plausibly drives tumor programs (genomic instability, altered signaling/metabolism) that promote T cell dysfunction or checkpoint upregulation, with LAG3 as a candidate mediator.


# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** In silico TCGA analyses: stratify tumors by RASSF1A status (expression/methylation), TIL density, and LAG3; assess OS and interaction effects;

**Prediction 2:** In vitro: manipulate RASSF1A in breast cancer cell lines; evaluate genomic instability markers, neoantigen-related signaling, and co-culture with T cells to monitor activation/exhaustion markers (including LAG3 axis);

**Prediction 3:** In vivo: use RASSF1A-knockout murine models to measure TIL infiltration and LAG3 expression; test anti-LAG3 therapy for TIL reinvigoration and tumor control;


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
