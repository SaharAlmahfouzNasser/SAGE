# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Static tumor-centric biomarkers (genomic risk scores like Decipher, and molecular readouts such as PCA3 or f/tPSA) sometimes fail to predict which localized low-to-intermediate risk prostate cancers progress. The Ontologist synthesis suggests this failure may arise because the tumor immune microenvironment and systemic/metabolic modifiers change how genomic programs translate into clinical progression. 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on the Decipher GC path as the primary mechanistic anchor because it encodes tumor-intrinsic metastatic programs directly tied to patient outcomes; I use this to ask why identical Decipher risk does not always yield the same clinical course. 3) Tier 2 evidence supporting the claim: (a) immune-context signals (predictive immune-response markers) and imaging-derived immune readouts (TIL density) plausibly modulate whether Decipher-encoded metastatic programs are executed; (b) metabolic/inflammatory modifiers (spermine, EDIH) and dynamic biomarkers (PSA trajectories) can further blunt or amplify immune control and thus alter progression risk. 4) Endpoint choice rationale: PFI (progression-free interval) is selected because it is the most sensitive, clinically relevant endpoint for localized disease where overall survival is often long and insensitive; Decipher aims to predict biologic progression/metastasis, so PFI aligns mechanistically and statistically. 5) Deliberate exclusions and why: I excluded forming the hypothesis around multiple Tier1 markers simultaneously (e.g., combining PCA3, f/tPSA and Decipher in the core claim) to keep the question sharp and mechanistic; PCA3, f/tPSA, spermine, EDIH, PSA trajectories and broader metabolite biomarkers are placed as secondary_modifiers to be tested as effect modifiers rather than core anchors?

## Working Hypothesis

Primary hypothesis: In localized prostate cancer, high Decipher GC scores (metastasis-associated transcriptional programs) predict worse progression-free interval (PFI), but this risk is attenuated in tumors with high intratumoral TIL density, such that Decipher-high tumors with dense TILs have a longer PFI than Decipher-high tumors with low TILs. The Decipher GC × TIL density interaction significantly predicts PFI, indicating that immune surveillance modulates the expression/impact of metastasis programs. Secondary modifiers (PCA3, f/tPSA, spermine, EDIH, and other predictive immune markers) are tested as effect modifiers of the Decipher-TIL interaction but are not core anchors. All analyses use PFI as the primary endpoint, with OS and metastasis-free survival as secondary endpoints for sensitivity analyses.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Data: use TCGA-BRCA and independent PCa cohorts with available Decipher GC scores or construct a close surrogate from transcriptomic data; quantify TIL density via pathology (digital image analysis) or immune-cell deconvolution (e.g., CIBERSORT/xCell) from transcriptomics.

**Prediction 2:** Analysis: fit multivariable Cox proportional hazards models for PFI with Decipher GC (high vs low), TIL density (high vs low), and an interaction term (Decipher GC × TIL). Adjust for confounders (age, PSA at diagnosis, Gleason score, pathologic stage, treatment type).

**Prediction 3:** Validation: replicate findings in an independent dataset with similar variables; perform robustness checks for TIL measurement method (IHC vs computational estimation).


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
