# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlights a recurrent discordance in MIBC — some tumors show favorable noninvasive or immune-associated readouts (e.g., high TILs or MSH6-linked mutational signals) yet behave aggressively or fail immunotherapy. Why does immune presence or predicted immunogenicity sometimes not produce clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), high TGF-β signaling driven by TGFB1 activity creates a stromal-immune barrier that neutralizes the predictive value of high tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs) in tumors with MSH6 alterations. Specifically, MSH6-altered tumors with elevated TGFB1 signaling will exhibit poor response to immune checkpoint blockade (ICB) despite high TIL density, due to TGF-β–mediated immune exclusion and suppression of T-cell effector functions. In this context, tumors with low TGFB1 signaling but with MSH6 alterations and high TILs are more likely to respond to ICB. Therefore, the mechanism predicts a subset of MIBC patients in whom TGFB1-driven stromal remodeling overrides neoantigen load (from MSH6 alterations) and impedes ICB efficacy; this subset should benefit from combination therapy that co-targets TGF-β signaling and immune checkpoints. Secondary modifiers, including urine DNA methylation biomarkers and Immunoscore-like T-cell density/localization, will modulate the strength of the TIL–therapy response relationship and provide noninvasive/tissue-context readouts of TGFB activity and immune status. The primary endpoint is therapy_response to ICIs, with specific predictions: (a) TIL-high/MSH6-altered, high TGFB1 activity = reduced response; (b) TIL-high/MSH6-altered, low TGFB1 activity = comparatively better or variable response; (c) TGFB1 blockade plus ICI improves response in TGFB1-high, MSH6-altered contexts in preclinical models. The hypothesis remains grounded in Tier1 anchor genes (TGFB1, MSH6) and Tier2 readouts, avoiding speculative Tier3 markers.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
