# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlighted that some MIBC tumors show both strong immune-infiltration signatures (Immunoscore) and a TGF-β–driven fibrotic microenvironment, yet these tumors sometimes fail to benefit from immune-based therapies. The puzzle is: why does apparent immune presence (high TIL/Immunoscore) not translate into effective anti-tumor immunity in a subset of tumors?

## Working Hypothesis

In MIBC, high TIL density predicts variable therapy_response depending on TGF-β pathway activity: tumors with high TGF-β signaling (as indicated by elevated TGFBR2 expression and SMAD3 pathway activity) will show reduced therapy_response to immune checkpoint blockade due to TGF-β–driven stromal remodeling and immune exclusion, whereas tumors with similar TIL density but low TGF-β signaling will have higher therapy_response. Secondary modifiers—MSH6, SARIFA, PTGDS, and TAF—modulate this relationship by influencing tumor immunogenicity (MSH6), stromal architecture and fibroblast signaling (SARIFA, TAF), and lipid/inflammatory mediators (PTGDS). This framework defines a biomarker-informed stratification: patients with high TIL density and high TGF-β signaling represent the subset most likely to be non-responders to monotherapy, who may require approaches that target TGF-β–driven stromal barriers to achieve benefit.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
