# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Intravesical BCG provokes local immune activation and often induces lymphoid organization, yet many urothelial carcinomas with apparent immune infiltration/TLS fail to achieve durable responses—why does organized local immunity sometimes not translate into BCG sensitivity? 2) Tier 1 paths used: the path linking PI3-kinase pathway activation → PD-L1 (CD274) and the path describing BCG → upregulation of PD-L1 provide a mechanistic backbone: tumor-intrinsic PI3K activity can induce PD-L1, and BCG itself can further elevate PD-L1, both converging to suppress antitumor T cell function. I therefore focused on the PI3K→CD274 axis. 3) Tier 2 supporting evidence: nodes such as TMPRSS2/ERG and PSMA (stem-cell–like programs / lineage features) suggest tumor-intrinsic oncogenic contexts that may co-select for immune-evasive phenotypes and impact durability of response; these reinforce but do not define the core mechanism. 4) Endpoint choice rationale: therapy_response (BCG response) was chosen because the path centers on BCG-induced immune biology and PD-L1–mediated suppression; this endpoint is the most clinically actionable for hypothesis-driven intervention (combination therapy). 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded prostate-specific edges (e.g., androgen deprivation and TMPRSS2/ERG as a core anchor) from the primary claim because they are less directly relevant to intravesical BCG in urothelial carcinoma; PSMA and TMPRSS2/ERG are included as secondary_modifiers for exploratory stratification. I also treated the semantically odd edge suggesting PD-L1 upregulates BCG as an annotation artifact and instead used the biologically plausible relationships (BCG induces PD-L1; PI3K induces PD-L1)?

## Working Hypothesis

Tumor-intrinsic PI3K signaling activation via PIK3CA drives upregulation of PD-L1 (CD274) on urothelial carcinoma cells, dampening TLS-like lymphoid aggregate–associated T cell activity during intravesical BCG. Clinically, TLS-like aggregates in tumors with high PIK3CA activity and CD274 expression will show reduced response to BCG, but concurrent PI3K or PD-L1 blockade with BCG is predicted to enhance TLS-driven anti-tumor immunity and improve outcomes.
