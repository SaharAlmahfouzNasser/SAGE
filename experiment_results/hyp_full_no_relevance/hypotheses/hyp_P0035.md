# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlights tumors with TGF-β–driven fibrosis and GREM1/BMP-antagonism that are not being captured by current prognostic stratification — why does a fibrotic, pro‑metastatic microenvironment fail to produce a consistent signal for progression across patients? 2) Tier 1 paths used: (a) TGF-β → fibrogenic, immunosuppressive stroma (core link to tissue remodeling); (b) GREM1 → BMP antagonism associated with lymph‑node metastasis (core link to metastatic potential). I built on these because they provide a mechanistic axis connecting stromal remodeling to enhanced dissemination. 3) Tier 2 evidence supporting the claim: MSH6 introduces a genomic‑instability/immunogenicity modifier that could interact with stromal effects; PSA/pro‑PSA and SARIFA point to cross‑modal (biochemical/imaging/histologic) signals that can complement stromal readouts; SDC2/TFPI2 methylation suggests an orthogonal, noninvasive epigenetic detection route. These support testing whether microenvironmental imaging features plus molecular signals improve prognostication. 4) Why PFI as the endpoint: GREM1’s link to lymph‑node metastasis and TGF‑β’s role in invasion imply an effect on recurrence/progression timing more directly than on long-term OS (which is confounded by subsequent therapies); PFI is therefore the most sensitive clinical endpoint to detect effects of pro‑metastatic microenvironmental programs in available cohorts. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded cross‑disease grading constructs (e.g., Gleason) and broad, multi‑gene lists from the core hypothesis to preserve mechanistic clarity and meet the 1 imaging + ≤2 gene constraint. Epigenetic markers (SDC2/TFPI2) and genomic modifiers (MSH6), as well as PSA/pro‑PSA and SARIFA, are relegated to secondary_modifiers for follow‑up analyses rather than the primary mechanistic claim?

## Working Hypothesis

High stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) indicates a TGFB1-driven fibrotic microenvironment that cooperates with GREM1-mediated BMP antagonism to establish a pro-metastatic niche facilitating early dissemination. In breast cancer, tumors with high TSR alongside elevated TGFB1 and GREM1 expression will show shorter progression-free interval, and the TSR–TGFB1–GREM1 signature will provide additive prognostic value beyond TSR alone.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Retrospective cohort analysis across breast cancer datasets with quantified TSR from digital pathology, TGFB1 and GREM1 expression data, and reported PFI; use multivariable Cox models with interaction terms to test whether TSR combined with TGFB1 and GREM1 improves prognostic performance over TSR alone.

**Prediction 2:** In vitro 3D co-culture systems pairing breast cancer cells with fibroblasts engineered to overexpress TGFB1 and GREM1; assess ECM remodeling, invasion, and response to TGF-β/BMP pathway modulators.

**Prediction 3:** In vivo orthotopic breast cancer models to evaluate metastasis in relation to TSR, TGFB1, and GREM1 status and to test the effect of pathway inhibitors on nodal/hematogenous spread.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
