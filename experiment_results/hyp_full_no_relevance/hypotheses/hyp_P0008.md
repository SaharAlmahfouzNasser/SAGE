## Clinical Question

I built on these because they directly frame the paradox of immune presence (TIL density) vs. functional response (therapy outcome) and provide a systemic (bTMB) vs. local (PD-L1, TILs) tension. 3) Tier 2 evidence supporting the claim: LRPPRC implicates tumor metabolic/mitochondrial programs that can suppress T cell function; MSH6 (DNA repair) can modulate neoantigen quality/quantity and thus alter the nature of immune priming; SARIFA and systemic metabolic markers (ALP/HDL-C/TG/SHBG/IGF-1) provide microenvironmental and host-state modifiers that could render TILs dysfunctional despite numeric density. 4) Why choose therapy_response as the endpoint: the puzzle concerns failure of immune infiltration to yield clinical benefit under PD-1/PD-L1 blockade, so a therapy-response proxy is the most direct, actionable endpoint for testing mechanism and informing combination strategies. 5) What I deliberately EXCLUDED and why: I limited the core hypothesis to one imaging feature (TIL density) plus two anchor molecular features (PD-L1 and bTMB) to keep the mechanistic question sharp; other Tier 2 modifiers (LRPPRC, MSH6, SARIFA, metabolic panel) are listed as secondary_modifiers for follow-up testing. Tier 3/exploratory paths were excluded from the core to avoid diluting the mechanistic focus?

## Working Hypothesis

Primary hypothesis: In muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), tumors that exhibit high intratumoral T cell density and PD-L1 expression but elevated tumor mutational burden (bTMB) respond poorly to PD-1/PD-L1 blockade due to concomitant TIL dysfunction driven by tumor-intrinsic metabolic programs and systemic host factors. This dysfunction is characterized by T cell exhaustion, impaired cytotoxicity, and an immunosuppressive metabolic microenvironment, which blunt reversal of suppression by checkpoint blockade. Secondary hypotheses: (i) Tumor upregulation of LRPPRC promotes mitochondrial metabolic rewiring that yields a lactate-rich, nutrient-depleted microenvironment, contributing to TIL dysfunction and resistance to PD-1/PD-L1 therapy; (ii) DNA repair context (MSH6 status) shapes neoantigen quality/quantity and presentation, modulating initial T cell priming such that high bTMB with unfavorable neoantigen landscapes correlates with non-response unless metabolic restraining signals are mitigated; (iii) Systemic metabolic host state, as reflected by serum markers (ALP, HDL-C, TG, SHBG, IGF-1) and SARIFA-positivity, correlates with an immunosuppressive milieu that further impairs TIL function and therapy response; (iv) A composite biomarker combining TIL density, PD-L1, bTMB, MSH6 status, tumor LRPPRC expression, and systemic metabolic markers will more accurately predict PD-1/PD-L1 therapy response and identify patients who require combination strategies targeting tumor metabolism and/or DNA repair–associated pathways to restore effective antitumor immunity.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** At the tissue level, tumors with the imaging feature but low anchor gene activity will show immune cells confined to structural peripheries rather than infiltrating tumor nests.

**Prediction 2:** In functional experiments, restoring or enhancing the anchor gene's signaling will improve immune-mediated tumor control in imaging-feature-positive models.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
