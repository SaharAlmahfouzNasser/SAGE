# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

TLS-like lymphoid aggregates in bladder tumors predict improved response to immune checkpoint inhibitors only when MSH6-dependent antigen processing and presentation are functionally intact in MSI/dMMR tumors. Specifically, TLS-rich MSI/dMMR bladder cancers with preserved MSH6 function will exhibit higher therapy_response rates to ICIs than TLS-rich MSI/dMMR tumors with MSH6 dysfunction or TLS-poor tumors, indicating that TLS presence alone is insufficient without competent antigen presentation. Secondary modifiers modulate this effect: TGFB1 signaling can suppress TLS efficacy and immune activation; PTEN loss fosters immune evasion; methylation of SDC2 and TFPI2 reflects epigenetic suppression of immune pathways; HDL-C represents systemic inflammatory/metabolic milieu; and circulating tumor cell burden (CTC_burden) reflects tumor burden and dynamics of immune surveillance. Therefore, the overall predictive model for ICI response in bladder cancer should integrate (1) anchor status (MSH6 expression/function and MSI/dMMR), (2) TLS presence, and (3) secondary modifiers (TGFB1, PTEN, SDC2/TFPI2 methylation, HDL-C, CTC_burden). This yields testable hypotheses about differential therapy_response rates and suggests combinatorial strategies (e.g., targeting TGF-β or PI3K/PTEN pathways) in TLS-rich/MSI tumors with compromised antigen presentation.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
