# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlighted that non-invasive urine methylation, tumor mutational/neoantigen load (bTMB) and immune-contexture (Immunoscore) converge but do not fully explain why some MIBC tumors with apparently favorable immune contexture fail to respond to immunotherapy. The core question is: under what tumor-intrinsic or microenvironmental conditions do organized lymphoid structures (or high local T-cell content) fail to translate into checkpoint-blockade sensitivity?

## Working Hypothesis

Primary hypothesis: In muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), TLS-like lymphoid aggregates reflect organized intratumoral immunity, but their anti-tumor potential is impeded when tumor-derived S100A9 skews myeloid cells toward a PD-L1–high, T cell–suppressive phenotype; accordingly, TLS-high tumors with high S100A9 and PD-L1 will show inferior response to PD-(L)1 blockade compared with TLS-high tumors with low S100A9. The S100A9–PD-L1 axis represents a targetable resistance mechanism, and modulating S100A9 signaling or PD-L1 within TLS-rich microenvironments should restore TLS-driven T cell activity and improve immunotherapy response. Secondary modifiers such as TP53 status or metabolic cues will tune the magnitude of this effect.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Retrospective and prospective cohorts to correlate TLS density/maturation and S100A9/PD-L1 expression within TLS regions with objective response to PD-(L)1 therapy;

**Prediction 2:** multiplex imaging and spatial transcriptomics to map S100A9 and PD-L1 within TLS microdomains;

**Prediction 3:** ex vivo TLS organoid/co-culture systems or humanized mouse models to test effects of S100A9 inhibition or PD-L1 blockade on T cell activation in TLS-rich contexts;


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
