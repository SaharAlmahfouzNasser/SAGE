# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

CEACAM5 is upregulated in tumors and associated with higher immune cell infiltration and MSI-H, yet increased TILs do not always translate into better patient outcomes. Why does CEACAM5-associated immune infiltration sometimes fail to confer a survival benefit? 2) Tier 1 paths used: (a) Tumors → CEACAM5 (CEACAM5 upregulation in tumors) and (b) CEACAM5 → Higher immune cell infiltration (association with increased immune infiltrate). These form the mechanistic backbone linking a tumor-expressed molecule to an immune-rich microenvironment. 3) Tier 2 support: the MSI-H association with higher immune infiltration supports the idea that CEACAM5-high tumors may overlap with immunogenic (MSI-H) biology; gene expression as the measurement modality supports testing transcriptional correlates of immune function. 4) Endpoint choice: Overall survival (OS) was selected because it is the most robust, widely available clinical endpoint in TCGA and best for testing whether immune infiltration associated with CEACAM5 yields a meaningful clinical benefit. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded the imaging modality 'ultrasound' and the organ context 'bladder' because they are peripheral to the central tumor-immune mechanism; I also excluded specific immune-exhaustion or MMR genes (e.g., PDCD1, MLH1) to keep the hypothesis focused on the CEACAM5–TIL axis, placing MSI-H into secondary_modifiers rather than the core claim?

## Working Hypothesis

CEACAM5 upregulation in tumor cells is associated with increased tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) density, an effect amplified in MSI-H tumors. However, CEACAM5-expressing tumors drive a qualitatively impaired TIL phenotype (e.g., higher exhaustion/regulatory markers and lower cytotoxic function), such that high TIL density does not translate into improved overall survival (OS). Specifically, CEACAM5-high/TIL-high/MSI-H tumors will show OS similar to or worse than CEACAM5-low/TIL-high/MSI-H tumors, and will exhibit higher expression of TIL exhaustion markers (e.g., PD-1, LAG-3, CTLA-4) and lower cytotoxic effector signals (e.g., GZMB, PRF1) within the TIL compartment. This interaction between CEACAM5 expression, TIL functional state, and MSI-H status will modulate survival and, in contexts with available therapy-response proxies, immunotherapy responses, such that CEACAM5-high tumors show attenuated benefit from immune checkpoint inhibitors despite high TIL density.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.
- Multi-modal validation pipeline: computational -> spatial -> functional, providing mechanistic depth beyond association.

## Caveats & Risks

- Hypothesis is computationally derived and requires experimental validation.

## Alternative Directions

- MSI-H: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
