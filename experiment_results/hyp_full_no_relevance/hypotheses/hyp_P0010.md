# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist flagged that MIBC tumors with apparent local immune organization (high Immunoscore/TLS or TILs) do not uniformly show favorable outcomes — why does immune presence sometimes fail to control progression? 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on the TGFB1 → stromal/fibrogenic remodeling path (Core Path 1) because TGF-β can create a physical and suppressive stromal niche, and on the immune-context/Immunoscore axis (Core Path 2) using CD8A as a marker of effector T cells/TLS effectiveness; these directly address how stroma and immune infiltration interact. 3) Tier 2 support: PTGDS (lipid/inflammatory modulation), SDC2/TFPI2 (epigenetic detectability in non-invasive samples), SARIFA (stromal–adipocyte niche) and ABH glycan remodeling all provide plausible secondary mechanisms that could modulate TLS function or offer alternative assay modalities, reinforcing the core stroma–immune interaction. 4) Endpoint rationale: I selected progression-free interval (PFI) because the puzzle concerns early failure/progression despite immune infiltration — PFI best captures clinically meaningful early tumor escape driven by local microenvironmental suppression. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded AI/translation-aspect, non-invasive assay modalities and broader multi-gene panels from the core hypothesis to keep the mechanistic question sharp; those elements are retained as secondary_modifiers for downstream biomarker development rather than being in the core mechanistic claim?

## Working Hypothesis

In muscle-invasive bladder cancer, TLS-rich tumors with high TGFB1 expression exhibit impaired CD8A+ T cell effector infiltration and function due to TGF-β–driven stromal remodeling that disrupts TLS architecture and excludes effector T cells. Consequently, TLS-high/TGFB1-high tumors will have shorter progression-free interval than TLS-high/TGFB1-low tumors, with the magnitude of this effect moderated by secondary modifiers PTGDS, SDC2, TFPI2, ABH, and SARIFA that reflect additional immune-regulatory or stromal cues and can serve as biomarkers for stratification or combination therapies.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
