# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlighted that dMMR/MSI and MSH6 are central to immunogenicity in bladder cancer, yet not all tumors with immune infiltration (or genomic instability signals) respond to immune checkpoint blockade — why do TIL-rich tumors sometimes fail to benefit? 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built primarily on the dMMR/MSI→ICI predictive axis (Core Path 1) and the MSH6 prognostic association (Core Path 2). These provide a mechanistic backbone linking mismatch-repair driven neoantigenicity to productive anti-tumor immunity. 3) Tier 2 evidence: supporting paths (HDL-C/metabolic state, AI-enabled biomarker accessibility, PTEN/tumor-suppressor interactions, and liquid-biopsy CTCs) suggest modulatory influences on immune efficacy and practical ways to deploy/monitor a combined biomarker (e.g., metabolic milieu may suppress TIL function; CTCs enable dynamic tracking). 4) Endpoint rationale: I chose a therapy_response endpoint (immune checkpoint inhibitor response) because the core mechanistic question concerns why genomic immunogenicity (dMMR/MSI/MSH6) translates variably into clinical benefit from ICIs — this is the most direct, actionable clinical consequence of the mechanisms identified. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded exploratory/cross-cancer signals (SARIFA, ADNP/PTGDS, DPYD) from the core claim to keep the hypothesis tightly mechanistic and testable; all additional candidate modulators (PTEN, HDL-C, TGF-β, CTCs) are listed as secondary_modifiers for follow-up analyses rather than embedded in the hypothesis sentence?

## Working Hypothesis

Tumor-Infiltrating Lymphocyte (TIL) density predicts immune checkpoint inhibitor (ICI) response in bladder cancer only when true mismatch repair deficiency (dMMR/MSI), as indicated by loss or dysfunction of MSH6; in MSH6-proficient or MSS tumors, high TIL density corresponds to non-productive or exhausted TILs and fails to predict benefit. Therefore, integrating TIL density with MSH6/dMMR/MSI status will yield superior prediction of therapy_response to ICIs over TIL density alone.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
