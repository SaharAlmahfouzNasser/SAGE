# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In bladder cancer, high stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) reflects TGF-β–driven fibroblast activation that creates a physical and immunosuppressive barrier, diminishing cytotoxic T cell infiltration and reducing immune checkpoint blockade (ICI) efficacy. MSI/dMMR-high tumors, by contrast, harbor elevated neoantigen loads that can partially overcome stromal exclusion, resulting in variable but potentially improved ICI responses. Therefore, a combined assessment of TSR (via TGFB1-driven signaling and stromal content) and MSI/dMMR status will identify subgroups with distinct ICI responses: stromal-high/MSI-stable tumors will be least responsive, stromal-high/MSI-high tumors will have intermediate responsiveness, and stromal-low tumors will show the strongest responses, with the potential for synergy if TGF-β pathway inhibitors are added to ICI in the stromal-high groups.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** retrospective multi-omics analysis of bladder cancer cohorts to correlate TSR (from pathology and transcriptomic TGF-β signatures) and MSI/dMMR with ICI outcomes;

**Prediction 2:** spatial and single-cell profiling to map CAF subtypes, TGF-β activity, and immune cell localization;

**Prediction 3:** in vitro co-culture and organoid models manipulating TGF-β signaling and MSI status to test effects on T cell cytotoxicity;


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
