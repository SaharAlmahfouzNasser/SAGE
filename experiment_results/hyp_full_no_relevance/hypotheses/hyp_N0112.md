# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

High stromal-to-tumor ratio defines a microenvironment that increases MTOR pathway dependence in bladder cancer; MTOR inhibition induces autophagy-driven cytotoxicity in TP53-wildtype tumors, whereas TP53-mutant tumors redirect autophagy toward survival, reducing therapeutic efficacy. Clinically, TSR-high, MTOR-dependent, TP53-wildtype bladder cancers will display improved overall survival under MTOR-targeted therapy compared with TSR-low or TP53-mutant tumors.
