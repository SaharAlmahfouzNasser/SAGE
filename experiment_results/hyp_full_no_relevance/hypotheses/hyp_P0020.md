# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

NMIBC tumors often show abundant intratumoral lymphocytes yet vary widely in progression; why does high TIL density sometimes fail to translate into durable tumor control? 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on PSMD2 (proteostasis/proteasome regulation of p53 and antigen-processing) and BRG1 (SWI/SNF chromatin remodeling that can repress antigen-presentation and chemokine programs). These two provide complementary mechanisms that can jointly disable T-cell–mediated control despite TIL presence. 3) Tier 2 evidence supporting the claim: metabolic enzymes (ADH4) and translational/ ncRNA programs can alter redox/antigenicity and cytokine/chemokine outputs; CONUT score captures host nutritional–immune competence that modulates whether intratumoral lymphocytes are effective; pluripotency/PIK3CA–linked stemness can increase immune evasion. 4) Why PFI: progression-free interval directly reflects NMIBC progression/recurrence risk (the clinical problem implied by the Ontologist) and is sensitive to incomplete immune control, making it the most appropriate single endpoint. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded exploratory Tier 3 signals (STMY3, chr10 q11.2 locus, AL357033.4 lncRNA) from the core hypothesis because they lack mechanistic specificity; I also limited core anchors to two genes (PSMD2, BRG1) to keep the hypothesis sharply testable, moving GARS1 and other Tier2 modifiers into secondary_modifiers for follow-up analyses?

## Working Hypothesis

Among NMIBC tumors with high TIL density, progression risk is elevated when concurrent PSMD2-driven proteasomal activity and BRG1-mediated chromatin remodeling suppress effective antigen processing/presentation and dampen chemokine/MHC signals necessary for cytotoxic T cell function. This core axis creates an immune-evading niche in which TILs are present but functionally impaired, leading to shorter progression-free interval (PFI). The effect is modulated by Tier2 modifiers: elevated GARS1 (translational/proteostatic flux), ADH4-driven metabolic/redox states, ncRNA programs that regulate immune and antigen-presentation genes, CONUT-detected nutritional/immune competence, and PIK3CA-driven PI3K signaling that promotes immune evasion. Collectively, tumors with high PSMD2 and BRG1 activity and adverse Tier2 modifier profiles will exhibit reduced MHC class I expression, diminished TAP1/LMP2 function, lower CXCL9/CXCL10 chemokine outputs, and decreased T cell cytotoxicity, translating into poorer PFI despite abundant TILs. In contrast, tumors with lower PSMD2/BRG1 activity or favorable Tier2 modifiers should show restored antigen presentation, enhanced T cell–mediated killing, and longer PFIs. This leads to testable predictions about molecular readouts, immune function assays, and clinical outcomes that can be evaluated in NMIBC cohorts and mechanistic models.
