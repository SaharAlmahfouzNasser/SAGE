# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), loss of E-cadherin and the broader EMT program (EMT-high state) reprogram the tumor immune microenvironment such that high tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) density reflects a predominantly peritumoral or dysfunctional T cell population with impaired cytotoxicity, rather than a productive intratumoral anti-tumor response. Consequently, EMT-high tumors with high TIL density will exhibit attenuated benefit from pembrolizumab monotherapy compared with EMT-low tumors with similar TIL densities, despite comparable PD-L1 status or tumor mutational burden. Mechanistically, EMT fosters T cell exclusion and functional impairment through: (a) ECM/fibroblast-rich stroma and altered chemokine landscapes favoring suppressive myeloid cells and regulatory T cells; (b) upregulation of inhibitory pathways and exhaustion markers on intratumoral T cells; (c) potential modulation of PD-L1 expression via EMT-associated signaling (e.g., TGF-β, ZEB1) but with the dominant effect being spatial and functional impairment of T cells rather than PD-L1 abundance alone. This EMT-TIL interaction should be detectable by (i) EMT status (E-cadherin loss and EMT gene signature) correlating with TIL localization (intratumoral vs peritumoral) and functional status (GZMB, IFN-γ, PD-1, TIM-3), (ii) imaging features of lesion morphology and invasion context (ground-glass nodules, CIS) providing contextual timing of EMT development, and (iii) clinical outcomes to pembrolizumab. The working prediction: TIL density is a positive predictor of pembrolizumab response only in EMT-low tumors; in EMT-high tumors, high TIL density does not translate to improved response or longer event-free survival.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Retrospective NSCLC cohorts treated with pembrolizumab with available tumor tissue; quantify EMT status via E-cadherin IHC and EMT gene expression, quantify TIL density and spatial localization by multiplex IHC/IF, assess T cell functionality (GZMB, IFN-γ, PD-1, TIM-3); perform multivariable analyses with PD-L1 and TMB as covariates and test TIL-EMT interaction.

**Prediction 2:** Spatial transcriptomics to map chemokine/ECM programs and immune cell states relative to EMT status.

**Prediction 3:** Preclinical validation using NSCLC cell lines/ORGs with E-cadherin knockdown/overexpression co-cultured with cytotoxic T cells and/or treated with PD-1 blockade; in vivo models with EMT induction/inhibition to assess TIL localization and response.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.
- Multi-modal validation pipeline: computational -> spatial -> functional, providing mechanistic depth beyond association.

## Caveats & Risks

- Hypothesis is computationally derived and requires experimental validation.

## Alternative Directions

- PD-L1: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
- ground-glass nodules (GGN): Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
