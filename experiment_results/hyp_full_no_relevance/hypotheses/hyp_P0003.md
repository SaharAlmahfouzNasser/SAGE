# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), high intratumoral TIL density indicates immune presence but does not guarantee response to PD-1/PD-L1 blockade because S100A9 signaling from tumor and myeloid cells drives adaptive upregulation of PD-L1 and induces T cell exhaustion. Consequently, TIL-high tumors that are also S100A9-high and PD-L1-high will exhibit reduced objective therapy_response to checkpoint inhibitors despite high bTMB or MSI/dMMR, whereas TIL-high tumors with low S100A9/PD-L1 will have a higher likelihood of benefit; urine-DNA methylation biomarkers may serve as a noninvasive surrogate to monitor and predict this mechanistic state and response trajectory.


| Element | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Imaging feature | Tumor-Infiltrating Lymphocyte (TIL) density |
| Anchor gene(s) | S100A9, PD-L1 |
| Primary endpoint | therapy_response |

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** provides a mechanistic scaffold to explain this discordance. Secondary modifiers (bTMB, MSI/dMMR, urine-DNA methylation) add context and enable noninvasive monitoring.

**Prediction 2:** In retrospective cohort data, the prognostic benefit of the imaging feature will be modified by the anchor gene expression level (significant interaction term).

**Prediction 3:** At the tissue level, tumors with the imaging feature but low anchor gene activity will show immune cells confined to structural peripheries rather than infiltrating tumor nests.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.

## Caveats & Risks

- Hypothesis is computationally derived and requires experimental validation.

## Alternative Directions

- bTMB: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
- MSI/dMMR: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
