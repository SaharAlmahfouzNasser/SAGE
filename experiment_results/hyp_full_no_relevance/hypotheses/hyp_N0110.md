## Working Hypothesis

In breast tumors with high tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) density, pro-apoptotic priming via BCL2L11 (Bim) enables CD8+ T cell–mediated tumor cell killing and improved overall survival (OS). However, concomitant S100A9-driven inflammatory signaling that activates MTOR-dependent pathways creates an immunosuppressive/metabolic milieu that blunts Bim-dependent apoptosis and attenuates TIL effectiveness. Consequently, among TIL-high tumors, OS will be highest in samples with high Bim and low S100A9/MTOR activity, intermediate in samples with discordant Bim/S100A9/MTOR levels, and lowest when S100A9/MTOR signaling dominates despite high TIL density. CD44, MTOR, and TGFB1 will modulate this relationship by shaping immune cell recruitment, metabolic support for tumor cells, and T cell exhaustion. Therefore, the prognostic benefit of high TIL density on OS is contingent on the balance between apoptotic priming (Bim) and S100A9/MTOR–driven inflammatory suppression; pharmacologic or genetic attenuation of S100A9 or MTOR signaling could restore TIL-associated OS advantages.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** observational validation in datasets with OS and expression data for BCL2L11, S100A9, MTOR pathway components, and TIL quantification;

**Prediction 2:** mechanistic in vitro experiments (co-culture of tumor cells with CD8+ T cells, modulation of Bim and S100A9/MTOR signaling, measurement of apoptosis and cytotoxicity); and

**Prediction 3:** in vivo modeling to test whether MTOR inhibition or S100A9 pathway blockade rescues TIL-mediated tumor control and improves OS. Primary endpoint remains OS; imaging feature is TIL density; anchor genes are BCL2L11 and S100A9; secondary modifiers are CD44, MTOR, and TGFB1.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
