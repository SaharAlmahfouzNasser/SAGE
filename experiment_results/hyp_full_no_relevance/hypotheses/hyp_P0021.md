# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlights that NMIBC shows heterogeneous biomarker signals—some tumors exhibit organized local immune structures (e.g., TLS/immunoscore) yet still recur or fail BCG; conversely, some tumors with apparent immune activation respond well. Why does organized local immunity sometimes fail to produce clinical benefit after BCG? 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built primarily on (a) the ADNP mRNA in CTCs path (tumor-intrinsic transcriptional programs and dissemination) and (b) the BCG → PD-L1 ←→ bTMB immunomodulatory axis (BCG-driven inflammation, PD-L1 induction, and mutational context). These converge on a mechanistic puzzle where tumor-intrinsic programs could subvert otherwise favorable local immune organization. 3) Tier 2 evidence supporting the claim: Immunoscore/immune infiltration metrics validate that organized immunity matters but are insufficient alone; MSH6/bTMB and mismatch-repair signals provide mutational context that can modulate PD-L1/neoantigen dynamics; non-invasive methylation markers (SDC2/TFPI2) illustrate feasibility of complementary liquid/tissue biomarkers. 4) Why therapy_response endpoint: Predicting BCG (intravesical immunotherapy) response is the highest-impact clinical problem in NMIBC—triaging patients for escalation (checkpoint blockade, early cystectomy) or alternative strategies. 5) What I excluded and why: I deliberately excluded ER status and TP53 from the core mechanistic claim to keep the hypothesis sharply focused (max 2 anchors) and because their roles are supportive/orthogonal; bTMB and MSH6 are listed as secondary modifiers rather than anchors to avoid conflating mutational-context variables with the core ADNP→PD-L1 mechanistic axis. I also excluded alternative imaging features (TIL density, TSR) to keep one precise imaging readout (TLS-like aggregates)?

## Working Hypothesis

In NMIBC, TLS-like lymphoid aggregates establish a locally active antitumor immune milieu, but tumor-intrinsic ADNP-driven transcriptional programs in urothelial cancer cells, when exposed to BCG-induced inflammation, upregulate and stabilize PD-L1 on tumor and stromal cells. This PD-L1 upregulation within TLS regions functionally suppresses effector CD8+ T cell activity, effectively converting TLS into an immunosuppressive niche and leading to BCG resistance despite apparent TLS maturation. Consequently, TLS-rich tumors that are ADNP-high and PD-L1-high will exhibit poor response to intravesical BCG, whereas TLS-rich tumors with low ADNP activity or lower PD-L1 will respond better. Mutational context (e.g., bTMB and MSH6 status) will modulate the magnitude of ADNP-driven PD-L1 induction and neoantigen visibility, contributing to inter-patient variability. Clinically, an integrated biomarker signature combining TLS status, ADNP expression (tumor and circulating tumor cells), and PD-L1 levels will stratify NMIBC patients for early escalation to PD-1/PD-L1 blockade or alternative/interventional strategies, while suggesting potential to target the ADNP-driven transcriptional program to restore TLS efficacy.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
