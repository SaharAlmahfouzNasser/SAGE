## Working Hypothesis

In NMIBC, the predictive value of TLS-like lymphoid aggregates for intravesical BCG response is stratified by MSH6 and MSI/dMMR status. Specifically, TLS presence in MSH6-proficient/MSI-stable tumors associates with favorable or neutral BCG responses, whereas in MSH6-deficient and/or MSI/dMMR tumors TLS are remodeled into dysfunctional or tolerogenic niches (e.g., T cell exhaustion, higher adaptive checkpoint signaling), leading to reduced BCG efficacy. Therefore, TLS alone is not a universal predictor of BCG response; the adverse impact of TLS is amplified in the MSH6-dMMR/MSI-high context. Consequently, NMIBC patients with MSH6-dMMR/MSI-high tumors represent a subset that may benefit from combination strategies (e.g., BCG plus PD-1/PD-L1 or CTLA-4 blockade or targeted TLS-supportive interventions) to restore TLS function and improve outcomes. This hypothesis is testable by correlating TLS architecture/function with BCG response across MSH6/MSI-dMMR strata and by evaluating combination therapies in preclinical models tailored to these contexts.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Retrospective cohort analyses of NMIBC patients treated with BCG with documented TLS status by pathology, MSH6 IHC/isolation, and MSI/dMMR testing, correlating TLS quality with treatment response across MSH6/MSI-dMMR strata.

**Prediction 2:** Spatial/molecular TLS profiling (IHC/IF for CD3, CD8, CD20, CXCL13, PNAd; Treg markers; exhaustion markers like PD-1, TIM-3; MMR/MSI status) and transcriptomic assessments (TLS gene signatures) to define functional vs. dysfunctional TLS in each subgroup.

**Prediction 3:** Ex vivo/organotypic bladder tumor explant models from patients stratified by MSH6/MSI-dMMR to test BCG alone vs. BCG + checkpoint blockade, assessing T cell activation, cytokine milieu, and TLS integrity.
