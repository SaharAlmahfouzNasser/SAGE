# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlighted that lncRNA-mediated control of TGF-β signaling (TGFB2‑AS1), and a TP53/CTNNB1–miR‑183 stemness axis, converge to produce divergent prognoses in NMIBC — specifically, why tumors with apparently similar stromal or microenvironmental features show opposite clinical outcomes. 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on the TGFB2‑AS1 path (core lncRNA node modulating TGF‑β/EMT and stromal activation) and the TP53/CTNNB1→miR‑183→stemness path (CTNNB1 as a driver of cell‑intrinsic stemness/EMT programs). These two provide a clear mechanistic tension: stromal/TGF‑β regulation versus tumor‑intrinsic stemness. 3) Tier 2 support: JAK3 (immune/JAK–STAT signaling), HSPE1 (proteostasis/stress adaptation), OIP5 (stemness/cell cycle), and the MIS/hormonal axis offer plausible modifiers that can shape stromal activation, immune contexture, and tumor cell resilience, reinforcing the proposed mechanism by which stromal signals are either restrained or overcome. 4) Endpoint choice: Overall survival (OS) was chosen as the single primary endpoint because it is the most definitive clinical outcome available across cohorts and best captures the net effect of microenvironment–tumor interactions on patient prognosis. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded broad, ill‑defined 'hub gene' labels and dispersed Tier‑3 candidates (YWHAE, FUT3) from the core claim to preserve mechanistic clarity and to respect the 1 imaging + ≤2 anchor gene constraint; those broader network hubs and exploratory genes are retained for downstream secondary analyses rather than the primary hypothesis?

## Working Hypothesis

In TSR-high tumors, the prognostic impact is determined by the relative activity of tumor cell–intrinsic CTNNB1-driven stemness and stromal TGFB2-AS1–modulated TGF-β signaling. TGFB2-AS1 dampens TGF-β–driven stromal activation and EMT, while CTNNB1 promotes EMT and stemness; theNet effect on overall survival (OS) depends on which program dominates. Specifically, within TSR-high contexts: (a) TGFB2-AS1-high and CTNNB1-low tumors will exhibit the best OS due to restrained stromal remodeling and limited stemness; (b) TGFB2-AS1-low and CTNNB1-high tumors will exhibit the worst OS due to unrestrained TGF-β–driven stroma and strong tumor cell stemness; (c) TGFB2-AS1-high/CTNNB1-high tumors will show intermediate OS where CTNNB1-driven stemness partially offsets TGFB2-AS1-mediated restraint. In TSR-low tumors, the influence of TGFB2-AS1 and CTNNB1 is diminished, yielding comparatively intermediate OS with less pronounced interactions. Tier 2 modifiers (TP53, JAK3, HSPE1, OIP5) will modulate these effects by shaping immune contexture, proteostasis/stress responses, and cell cycle/stemness, refining risk stratification across TSR and anchor-gene strata. The primary endpoint is OS in TCGA cohorts, with secondary endpoints including progression-free survival and metastasis-free survival and supportive analyses using TIL/TLS density and immune signatures to link microenvironment context with genomic/epigenomic states.

- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
