# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Why do TLS-like lymphoid aggregates (histologic evidence of local immune organization) sometimes fail to confer progression-free benefit? The path suggests tumor-intrinsic PI3K signaling drives PD-L1 upregulation, which could neutralize TLS-mediated antitumor immunity. 2) Tier 1 paths used: (a) TMPRSS2/ERG cooperativity → PI3-kinase pathway activation; (b) PI3-kinase pathway activation → PD-L1 up-regulation. I built on these because they form a direct mechanistic axis linking oncogenic signaling to adaptive immune checkpoint expression. 3) Tier 2 supporting evidence: PSMA expression in stem-like tumor cells and the concept of cancer stem cell compartments provide a cellular reservoir that may sustain PI3K-driven, PD-L1-high clones resistant to immune control; androgen-deprivation/therapy-related selective pressures (from the path) offer a plausible context for selection of such signaling programs. 4) Endpoint choice: Progression-free interval (PFI) was chosen because the proposed mechanism (immune evasion via PD-L1 driven by PI3K) is expected to impact time to recurrence/progression more directly than long-term overall survival and because PFI is a robust, therapy-relevant clinical readout in TCGA-style cohorts. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded BCG/urothelial-specific nodes, prostate-organ context, and broader carcinoma/general cancer-cell nodes as out-of-scope for a focused mechanistic test in epithelial tumors; I also limited the hypothesis to one imaging feature and two core molecular anchors to maintain mechanistic specificity?

## Working Hypothesis

Mechanistic hypothesis: In breast cancer, the presence of TLS-like lymphoid aggregates is associated with longer progression-free interval (PFI) only when tumor cells do not exhibit PI3K pathway-driven PD-L1 upregulation. When PI3K pathway activation (represented by PIK3CA alterations or expression-based PI3K activity signatures) drives upregulation of PD-L1 (CD274) on tumor cells, TLS-like aggregates fail to confer TLS-associated anti-tumor immunity, resulting in shorter PFI. Therefore, TLS status, PD-L1 expression, and PI3K activation interact to shape the prognosis of breast tumors, with the strongest adverse prognosis observed in TLS-high, PD-L1-high, PI3K-active tumors compared with TLS-high, PD-L1-low or TLS-low tumors. Secondary modifiers TMPRSS2/ERG and PSMA signatures may modulate this axis as contextual factors but are not required anchors; they could reflect broader oncogenic signaling cross-talk that reinforces PD-L1 upregulation or immune exclusion in certain subsets. Experimental scope: (i) test the three-way interaction TLS × PD-L1 × PI3K on PFI using TCGA-BRCA data; (ii) validate TLS detection and PD-L1/PI3K activity readouts with orthogonal measures; (iii) exploratory analysis of TMPRSS2/ERG and PSMA signatures as potential modulators; (iv) if feasible, perform targeted in vitro assays to assess PD-L1 induction by PI3K pathway activation and its impact on T cell–mediated cytotoxicity in TLS-mimetic contexts.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
