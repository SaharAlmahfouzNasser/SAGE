# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

Core mechanistic claim: In NMIBC, high TIL density alone does not guarantee effective anti-tumor immunity because tumor-intrinsic S100P drives PI3K signaling that upregulates PD-L1, a process amplified in TP53-mutant tumors, leading to T cell exhaustion and impaired cytotoxic activity. Clinically, NMIBC tumors that are TIL-high and S100P-high with TP53 mutation will exhibit poorer response to BCG compared with TIL-high, S100P-low, TP53-wild-type tumors; integrating TIL density with S100P and TP53 status will more accurately stratify patients for BCG responsiveness. Supporting modifiers (e.g., ADNP as a secondary modifier and PD-L1/PI3K axis) may influence the strength of this interaction and could identify additional subgroups in need of combination therapies. Experimental readouts should include PD-L1 expression, PI3K pathway activity, and T cell functional assays in the context of S100P and TP53 status, correlated with therapy_response endpoints.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** in vitro bladder cancer models with defined TP53 status and manipulated S100P to measure PD-L1 and PI3K signaling and T cell cytotoxicity;

**Prediction 2:** ex vivo analysis of NMIBC specimens with known BCG outcomes to correlate TIL density, S100P, TP53 mutation, PD-L1, PI3K activity, and response;

**Prediction 3:** prospective biomarker analyses in clinical cohorts to quantify the added predictive value of combining these markers. This framework supports a testable, clinically actionable stratification approach for BCG therapy.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
