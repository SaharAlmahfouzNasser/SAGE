# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Many NMIBC tumors show dense TLS-like lymphoid aggregates yet still fail to achieve durable responses to intravesical BCG — why does local immune organization not uniformly translate into effective therapy response? 2) Tier 1 paths used and why: I built on Core Path 1 (BCG → biomarkers) to center the question on BCG response; Core Path 2 (urine DNA methylation biomarkers) to capture tumor-derived epigenetic signals that can reflect antigen-presentation or immune-evasive programs noninvasively; and Core Path 3 (ADNP mRNA in CTCs/liquid biopsy) to represent a circulating tumor transcript that may mark intrinsic tumor biology linked to immune suppression. 3) Tier 2 evidence supporting the claim: Immunoscore-like metrics (CD3/CD8 spatial densities) and PD-L1/bTMB/MSI provide orthogonal local and systemic measures of tumor immunogenicity that could modulate whether TLS mature into effective anti-tumor responses; AI-enabled integration supports practical combination of these modalities. 4) Why I chose therapy_response as the endpoint: BCG response is the immediate, clinically actionable outcome for NMIBC where biomarker-guided treatment escalation/de-escalation would change management; it's the most relevant endpoint for intravesical immunotherapy biomarker work. 5) What I deliberately excluded and why: I excluded exploratory Tier 3 paths (ER/TP53 interactions, stool methylation, systemic metabolic panels) because they are indirect to NMIBC-BCG immune biology and would dilute mechanistic focus; I also excluded alternative imaging features (TIL density, TSR) to keep a sharp mechanistic test of TLS functional competence versus tumor-intrinsic epigenetic/transcript programs?

## Working Hypothesis

Mechanistic claim: In TLS-rich NMIBC tumors, durable response to intravesical BCG is compromised when tumor-intrinsic epigenetic silencing, reflected by high urine DNA methylation burden, coexists with elevated ADNP mRNA in circulating tumor cells, leading to impaired antigen presentation and incomplete TLS maturation, thereby reducing local cytotoxic T cell (CTL) activation and BCG efficacy. Clinical consequence: A composite biomarker integrating TLS quality on H&E (beyond mere presence), urine DNA methylation burden, and ADNP mRNA levels will better stratify patients into likely BCG responders versus non-responders, enabling earlier treatment escalation or alternative immunotherapeutic approaches for those unlikely to benefit.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
