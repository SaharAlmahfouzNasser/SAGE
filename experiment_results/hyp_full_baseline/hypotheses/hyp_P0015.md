# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

From the Ontologist synthesis, the key puzzle is why some NMIBC tumors with apparent local immune organization (which should favor intravesical BCG efficacy) nonetheless fail to achieve durable BCG responses. Tier 1 paths suggest a triad of mechanisms: DNA-repair status (MSH6) → altered neoantigen/mutational landscape (bTMB) → immune-checkpoint engagement (PD-L1) that together modulate whether local immune structures are productive. 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on Core Path 1 (BCG–biomarker clinical-trial axis) to anchor the clinical context and Core Path 2 (bTMB ↔ PD-L1) and Core Path 3 (MSH6 as an MMR/prognostic factor) because they provide the mechanistic links between tumor-intrinsic mutational burden and local immune-suppression that plausibly determine BCG responsiveness. 3) Tier 2 support: Liquid-biopsy signals (ADNP in CTCs) and Immunoscore-like spatial immune metrics support assayability and orthogonal validation of the immune-priming state; TP53/ER and metabolic signals (HDL-C) offer secondary explanations for heterogeneity but are peripheral. 4) Why therapy_response endpoint: The clinical question is explicitly about BCG efficacy in NMIBC — a therapy-response proxy is the most direct, clinically actionable endpoint for intravesical immunotherapy and best connects mechanistic variables (TLS, MSH6, PD-L1) to intervention. 5) Exclusions and focus decisions: I deliberately excluded bTMB from the core variable list to keep the hypothesis sharp (MSH6 functions mechanistically upstream of bTMB and is included as a core anchor), but retained bTMB and other assay/readout candidates (ADNP, CTCs, Immunoscore) as secondary_modifiers for empirical follow-up. I also excluded exploratory, cross-domain paths (HDL-C, CSF AD biomarkers, stool methylation) from the core claim because their links to intravesical BCG response are indirect?

## Working Hypothesis

In NMIBC treated with intravesical BCG, TLS-like lymphoid aggregates predict higher therapy_response only when a mechanistic cascade is present: (1) MSH6 deficiency (or loss) increases tumor mutational burden/neoantigen landscape, enabling TLS-primed T cell activation; (2) PD-L1 expression is low to moderate, permitting effective TLS-driven cytotoxic activity, whereas high PD-L1 expression suppresses TLS-derived responses and diminishes BCG efficacy; (3) bTMB magnitude modulates the TLS–MSH6–PD-L1 axis by adjusting neoantigen density, with higher bTMB enhancing response in PD-L1-low contexts but not overcoming PD-L1-driven suppression at high levels. Consequently, TLS-high/MSH6-deficient/PD-L1-low tumors will show the highest therapy_response rates to BCG; TLS-high/PD-L1-high tumors will have attenuated responses despite organized lymphoid structures; TLS-low tumors will have poor responses irrespective of MSH6 status or PD-L1 level. Secondary modifiers (ADNP expression in CTCs, Immunoscore metrics, and TP53 status) will refine predicted outcomes and provide non-invasive or spatial validation of the primary mechanism, but are not required for the core TLS–MSH6–PD-L1 interaction to govern response.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Retrospective tissue/blood analyses from NMIBC cohorts treated with BCG to assess TLS, MSH6, PD-L1, bTMB, Immunoscore, ADNP-CTC, and TP53;

**Prediction 2:** Prospective validation with pre-BCG biopsies and matched blood samples, standardized TLS scoring (multiplex IF or digital pathology), MSH6 IHC/sequencing, PD-L1 IHC, and bTMB from a targeted panel;

**Prediction 3:** Preclinical mechanistic work in orthotopic NMIBC models with controlled MSH6 and PD-L1 manipulation to observe TLS formation and BCG response;


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
