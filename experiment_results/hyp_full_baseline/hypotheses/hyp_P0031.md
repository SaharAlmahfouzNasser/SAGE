# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlights that tumor transcriptomes and specific circRNAs (e.g., circMMP9) associate with cisplatin response, yet some tumors with favorable microenvironmental features (e.g., immune infiltration) still fail to respond. Why does a transcriptional/post-transcriptional program override microenvironmental predictors of cisplatin sensitivity?

## Working Hypothesis

Primary hypothesis: In bladder cancer, high eIF4E-driven cap-dependent translation increases circMMP9 biogenesis, and this circMMP9 promotes tumor-intrinsic resistance to cisplatin, effectively diminishing the predictive value of tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs). Consequently, tumors with high eIF4E and high circMMP9 will exhibit cisplatin resistance irrespective of high TIL density; disrupting circMMP9 or reducing eIF4E activity will restore cisplatin sensitivity. Secondary hypotheses: (i) circMMP9 acts as a sponge for miRNAs that regulate DNA damage response and apoptotic pathways, leading to attenuated cisplatin-induced DNA damage signaling; (ii) EIF4A3 and other splicing back-end factors modulate circMMP9 biogenesis and are necessary for the full resistant phenotype; (iii) circTRAM1 and related modifiers can modulate the axis but do not dominate the cisplatin response when eIF4E/circMMP9 are high; (iv) combining circMMP9 inhibition with cisplatin will have synergistic anti-tumor effects and better clinical predictivity than TIL metrics alone.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.

## Caveats & Risks
