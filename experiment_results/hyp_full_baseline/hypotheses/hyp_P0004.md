# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlights TGF-β signaling (TGFB1) and systemic lipid biology (HDL-C) as core, plus immune-contexture readouts (Immunoscore/TLS). The key puzzle is: why do TLS- or TIL-rich tumors sometimes fail to deliver the expected anti-tumor benefit? Specifically, could tumor-intrinsic fibrogenic signaling (TGF-β) and host lipid/metabolic state (HDL-C) interact to disrupt TLS maturation/function and permit early progression?

## Working Hypothesis

In breast cancer, TLS-like lymphoid aggregates confer a favorable progression-free interval (PFI) only when tumor TGFB1 signaling is low and systemic HDL-C status supports anti-tumor immunity. Elevated tumor TGFB1 signaling promotes extracellular matrix remodeling and recruitment of immunosuppressive myeloid cells, thereby disrupting TLS maturation and antigen presentation, which negates the TLS-associated PFI benefit. HDL-C modulates TLS biology and immune cell function; when HDL-C signaling is favorable, it may support TLS maintenance and effector T cell activity, but dysregulated HDL-C (aberrant HDL quality, particle number, or signaling signatures) can synergize with TGFB1 to impair TLS functionality. The interaction TLS_density × TGFB1_expression × HDL-C will be a primary testable axis predicting PFI: high TLS density predicts longer PFI only in TGFB1-low and HDL-C–favorable contexts; in TGFB1-high or HDL-C-disrupted contexts, TLS density loses its protective association or may correlate with shorter PFI due to impaired TLS maturation and pro-fibrotic microenvironments. Secondary considerations include how Immunoscore (CD3/CD8) and other TLS components (B cell zones, follicular dendritic cell markers) modulate this relationship, and whether effects differ by breast cancer subtype (e.g., HR+/HER2-, HER2+, triple-negative) or treatment context.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
