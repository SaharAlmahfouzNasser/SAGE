# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Why do some immune-infiltrated tumors (high TILs) fail to translate immune presence into better outcomes? The provided path links upstream inflammatory signaling (S100A9) to enhanced PD-L1-mediated immune suppression, and connects risk-factor biology to prognostic mediators (SPP1/Osteopontin) and FGFR3-associated favorable signals. 2) Tier 1 paths used: the S100A9 → PD-L1 axis (mechanistic bridge from inflammation to immune checkpoint upregulation) — chosen because it directly explains how an inflammatory program can neutralize anti-tumor lymphocyte activity. 3) Tier 2 evidence supporting the claim: EPB41L2 (risk-factor association) and Osteopontin/SPP1 (prognostic mediator) suggest risk-background and secreted inflammatory mediators may co-vary with S100A9-driven programs; FGFR3's link to good prognosis provides a counterpoint for stratification. 4) Endpoint choice: Overall survival (OS) — selected because it is the most robust, uniformly available clinical endpoint in TCGA-BRCA and best captures the ultimate clinical consequence of immune evasion. 5) Exclusions/deliberate simplification: I excluded SPP1 (Osteopontin), EPB41L2, and FGFR3 from the two-gene core hypothesis to keep the mechanistic question sharp; these are placed as secondary_modifiers for testing interactions/stratification. I also did not frame the hypothesis as an ICI-response prediction because TCGA lacks systematic immune-checkpoint therapy response data?

## Working Hypothesis

Expanded hypothesis: In breast cancers with high tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TIL-high), concurrent high tumor-intrinsic S100A9 expression drives upregulation of PD-L1 (CD274) on tumor and/or tumor-associated myeloid cells via TLR4/NF-κB signaling and inflammatory cues, generating an immunosuppressive microenvironment that blunts cytotoxic T cell function and reduces overall survival (OS) compared with TIL-high tumors lacking this S100A9–PD-L1 axis. This core axis is modulated by: (i) SPP1 (osteopontin) co-expression, which enhances myeloid recruitment and PD-L1 induction, amplifying immune suppression and worsening prognosis; (ii) EPB41L2 expression, which reflects risk-background transcriptomic context and alters the strength of the inflammatory–checkpoint program; (iii) FGFR3 signaling, which can counterbalance or rewire the axis in subtype-specific contexts, potentially yielding better prognosis in subsets. testable predictions and approaches: 1) In silico TCGA-BRCA analyses will show a significant S100A9 × CD274 interaction effect on OS within TIL-high tumors, independent of standard covariates and within molecular subtypes; 2) Spatial proteomics/imaging will reveal close spatial colocalization of S100A9+ myeloid cells with CD274+ cells and concurrent suppression of T cell effector markers (e.g., Granzyme B, IFN-γ) in tumors with high S100A9 and CD274; 3) In vitro co-culture systems using breast cancer cell lines and S100A9-activated myeloid cells will demonstrate PD-L1 upregulation on target cells and reduced CD8+ T cell cytotoxicity, reversible by PD-L1 blockade or S100A9 inhibition; 4) In vivo models will show that disrupting S100A9 signaling or PD-L1 blockade ameliorates the immunosuppressive phenotype and improves survival in TIL-rich tumors; 5) A composite risk score integrating S100A9, CD274, SPP1, EPB41L2, and FGFR3 will stratify OS beyond TIL density alone, with the axis' strength predicting differential response to checkpoint inhibition in retrospective cohorts.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
