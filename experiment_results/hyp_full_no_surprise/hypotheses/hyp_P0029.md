# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

From the Ontologist's synthesis, the most compelling question is why tumors that show signs of an active IFN-γ–driven immune program and histologic immune organization (e.g., TLS) nonetheless have variable clinical benefit — i.e., when and how does local immune organization fail to translate into durable tumor control? 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built the hypothesis on Core Path 1 (IFN-γ signaling / IRF1 axis) and Core Path 2 (chromatin remodeling defects, e.g., ARID1A), because these directly intersect: chromatin remodelers control accessibility of IRF1/IFN-γ target loci (chemokines, MHC), thereby determining whether TLSs are functionally mature. 3) Tier 2 support: SPHK1 (lipid/inflammation axis), ZNF687-AS1 (lncRNA), LCOR, and the proposed biomarker panel provide plausible upstream modulators or readouts that could tune chromatin/IFN responsiveness or TLS composition, reinforcing the mechanism that epigenetic or metabolic modifiers can render TLSs non-functional. 4) Why PFI as endpoint: progression-free interval best captures clinically meaningful failure of local immune control (short-term tumor progression) and is available and sensitive in the TCGA clinical data for evaluating immune-driven tumor control, unlike limited ICI-response labels. 5) What I deliberately excluded and why: I limited the core hypothesis to one imaging feature (TLS-like lymphoid aggregates) and two anchor genes (ARID1A, IRF1) to keep the mechanistic question sharp and testable; other IFN pathway members (IFNG, STAT1), additional remodelers (SMARCA4), and Tier 2 genes (PPARD, SPHK1, ZNF687-AS1, LCOR, CXCL9, CD8A) are placed as secondary_modifiers for follow-up rather than cluttering the core causal question?

## Working Hypothesis

Primary hypothesis: In tumors with TLS-like lymphoid aggregates, ARID1A deficiency constrains chromatin accessibility at IRF1/IFN-γ target loci (e.g., chemokines such as CXCL9/10/11, and antigen-presentation genes such as MHC I/II), yielding functionally immature TLSs that are structurally present but fail to sustain effective anti-tumor immunity, thereby shortening progression-free interval (PFI) despite TLS enrichment. Conversely, ARID1A-wildtype tumors with TLSs permit IRF1-driven transcription in accessible chromatin, promoting chemokine production, robust antigen presentation, effective CD8+ T cell trafficking and activation, mature TLS architecture, and longer PFI. The effect is modulated by Tier 2 factors (SPHK1, ZNF687-AS1, LCOR, PPARD) and broader chromatin/epigenetic context; manipulating IRF1-accessibility or ARID1A function can rescue TLS functionality and extend PFI. Testable predictions: (1) TLS-high/ARID1A-mutant tumors will have shorter PFI than TLS-high/ARID1A-wildtype; (2) IRF1 target gene expression, IRF1 occupancy, and chromatin accessibility at IRF1-responsive loci will be reduced in TLS-containing, ARID1A-deficient contexts; (3) Restoring ARID1A-dependent chromatin remodeling or enhancing IRF1 accessibility will restore TLS maturity and prolong PFI; (4) Modulators such as SPHK1/ZNF687-AS1/LCOR/PPARD will shift TLS functional status by altering chromatin responsiveness or immune effector recruitment. The framework integrates histologic TLS presence with functional genomic readouts to explain why a histologically organized immune milieu may not translate into durable tumor control in a subset of tumors.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
