# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Why do some tumors with favorable immune architecture still fail to achieve better clinical outcomes?

## Working Hypothesis

Primary hypothesis: In muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), the prognostic value of tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) density for progression-free interval (PFI) is governed by tumor PD-L1 (CD274) expression. Specifically, TIL-high tumors with CD274-high status will have shorter PFI than TIL-high tumors with CD274-low status, due to PD-L1–mediated suppression of CD8A+ cytotoxic T-cell activity; conversely, in CD274-low tumors, high TIL density will associate with longer PFI, reflecting effective anti-tumor immunity. Secondary modifiers: loss of HLA class I antigen presentation (e.g., B2M/HLA-A/B/C disruption) and SARIFA (tumor-adipocyte/stromal interface associated immune exclusion) will further blunt or modulate the protective effect of TILs, potentially attenuating the favorable impact of TIL-high status even when CD274 is low. Overall, the interaction between TIL density and CD274, modulated by HLA class I status and SARIFA, will provide a more accurate, mechanistic predictor of PFI than TILs alone in MIBC.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** Immunoscore-type measures reflect immune density but can be misleading if functional suppression via PD-L1 undermines anti-tumor activity;

**Prediction 2:** PD-L1 expression on tumor cells or within the microenvironment has been associated with immune evasion and variable prognosis across bladder cancer cohorts;

**Prediction 3:** HLA class I loss diminishes antigen presentation, reducing T-cell recognition and effectiveness;


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.

## Caveats & Risks

- Hypothesis is computationally derived and requires experimental validation.

## Alternative Directions

- HRR (hemoglobin, RDW): Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
- HLA-A / HLA-B / HLA-C (class I antigen presentation): Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
