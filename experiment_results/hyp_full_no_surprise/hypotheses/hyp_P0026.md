# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist emphasized liquid biopsy (CTCs) and MSI/AI-derived signatures as Tier 1 anchors and highlighted a translational tension: organized local immune structures (e.g., TLS) should predict benefit from immunotherapy, yet many immune-infiltrated tumors fail to respond. The core puzzle is: under what systemic or molecular conditions does local immune organization (TLS) fail to translate into therapy response?

## Working Hypothesis

In bladder cancer, TLS-like lymphoid aggregates predict higher objective response rates to immune checkpoint blockade (ICB) primarily when the tumor also harbors MSI (high neoantigen load) and has a low circulating tumor cell (CTC) burden. Specifically: (1) TLS-high, MSI-high, and CTC-low tumors will show the highest responses and longest progression-free intervals (PFI)/overall survival (OS) under ICB; (2) TLS-high but MSI-stable/MSS or TLS-high with high CTC burden will have attenuated responses due to lower neoantigenicity (MSI status) or systemic antigen sinks/immune evasion (CTC burden); (3) TLS-poor tumors will exhibit limited ICB benefit regardless of MSI or CTC status, as local immune scaffolding is insufficient to drive systemic anti-tumor activity. The primary testable prediction is a significant TLS x MSI x CTC interaction effect on therapy response, with the strongest signal in TLS-high, MSI-high, CTC-low tumors. Secondary predictions include: a) within TLS-high cohorts, MSI status and CTC burden independently modulate response; b) the predictive value of TLS is diminished in MSS/MSI-low contexts or in high-CTC contexts, supporting a stratified biomarker approach combining TLS with MSI and CTC burden to guide ICI therapy decisions.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
