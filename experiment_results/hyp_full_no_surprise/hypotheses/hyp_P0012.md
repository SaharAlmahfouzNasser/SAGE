# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlights a tension where tumor-intrinsic immunogenicity from MMR deficiency (MSH6) should sensitize tumors to immunotherapy, yet adipocyte-rich, stromal-remodeled tumors (SARIFA) and systemic lipid/metabolic states (HDL-C) may promote immune exclusion and metastatic behavior—why do some MSH6-deficient MIBC tumors fail to realize an immunogenic benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), MSH6 deficiency increases tumor neoantigen burden and baseline immunogenicity, but the observed immunotherapy response is critically shaped by the stromal–lipid microenvironment. Specifically, TSR-low tumors will permit enhanced T-cell infiltration and superior response to immune checkpoint blockade in the context of MSH6 deficiency, whereas TSR-high, adipocyte-rich stroma fosters a lipid-rich, TAF-mediated immune-excluding niche that blunts neoantigen presentation and T-cell infiltration, thereby diminishing therapy_response despite MSH6 deficiency. Elevated HDL-C is predicted to amplify this lipid-mediated immune exclusion, further reducing therapy_response in the TSR-high/MSH6-deficient subset. Therefore, the highest therapy_response is expected in tumors that are MSH6-deficient with low TSR and low HDL-C, and the lowest in tumors that are MSH6-deficient with high TSR and high HDL-C; intermediate outcomes are anticipated for other combinations. This hypothesis is testable by (1) measuring MSH6 status, TSR, and HDL-C in MIBC patients treated with ICIs and assessing therapy_response; (2) modeling interaction effects (MSH6 × TSR × HDL-C) on response, with adjustments for known confounders; and (3) validating mechanisms in relevant preclinical models (organoid/CAF co-cultures and MSH6-deficient mouse bladder cancer with diet-induced adiposity).

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
