# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist's path indicates PROS1 is expressed in tumors and is associated with Ki-67, implying PROS1-related signaling may drive or reflect proliferation; the key question is under what microenvironmental conditions PROS1 associates with increased proliferation and worse outcome. 2) Tier 1 paths used: (a) Tumor -> PROS1 (PROS1 expressed in tumor tissue) and (b) PROS1 -> Ki-67 association (links PROS1 to proliferation). I built on these because they form the simplest mechanistic backbone connecting a tumor-expressed ligand to a canonical proliferation readout. 3) Tier 2/supporting evidence: The ultrasound → gene expression non-effect supports that measured PROS1/MKI67 levels reflect biology rather than imaging artifact, and the organ/microenvironment context (bladder in the raw path) motivates testing how stromal context modulates PROS1's effect. 4) Why OS chosen: Overall survival is the most robust, clinically consequential endpoint available in TCGA and is the outcome most plausibly impacted by sustained increases in proliferation (MKI67) driven by microenvironment-modulated PROS1 signaling. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded additional molecular actors (e.g., specific TAM receptor gene names, coagulation cascade genes) to keep the hypothesis sharply focused on the PROS1→proliferation axis and an imaging-derived microenvironmental modifier; those factors are reserved for secondary or follow-up analyses because they were not enumerated as anchor genes in the provided Ontologist output?

## Working Hypothesis

PROS1 expression in breast tumor tissue is positively associated with MKI67 (Ki-67) levels, and this association is amplified in tumors with low stromal-to-tumor ratio (low TSR). Consequently, high PROS1 with low TSR predicts poorer overall survival (OS) compared with low PROS1 or high-TSR contexts. The effect is mediated by microenvironmental modulation of PROS1 signaling pathways that promote proliferation, resulting in a significant PROS1 × TSR interaction on MKI67 and a corresponding prognostic interaction on OS.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** PROS1 is a ligand that can engage TAM receptor signaling, potentially promoting proliferative programs in tumor cells;

**Prediction 2:** MKI67 is a robust readout of cell proliferation and is prognostically relevant in breast cancer;

**Prediction 3:** TSR captures stromal content relative to tumor cells and reflects the microenvironmental context that can modulate signaling and tumor behavior. Integrating PROS1 and MKI67 with TSR allows testing a context-dependent PROS1-driven proliferation hypothesis and its impact on OS.


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
