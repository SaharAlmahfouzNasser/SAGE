## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlighted that immune or molecular signals (e.g., immune infiltrates, MSH6 status, EV mRNA signatures) do not always translate into improved outcomes — i.e., why do some immune-infiltrated MIBC tumors still have poor prognosis? 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on MSH6 (canonical MMR gene whose alteration shapes mutational/neoantigen landscape) and pZVR (reported independent predictor of lymph node metastasis/metastatic propensity). HDL-C, PTGDS and exploratory CSF/PRAD links were omitted from the core hypothesis because they are either systemic/metabolic or cross-cancer analogies with weaker bladder-specific mechanistic support?

## Working Hypothesis

Primary hypothesis: In muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), the survival benefit associated with high tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) density is conditioned by MSH6 status. Specifically, in MSH6-proficient tumors, high TIL density predicts improved overall survival (OS). In contrast, in MSH6-deficient tumors with mismatch repair dysfunction, intratumoral neoantigen heterogeneity and clonal diversity promote metastasis-prone subclones, thereby negating or reversing the TIL-associated survival advantage and leading to equal or worse OS compared with TIL-low disease. This interaction is further modulated by Tier2 modifiers—KRT17, MDK, MSI status, Immunoscore-like metrics, and extracellular vesicle (EV) mRNA readouts—that reflect immune contexture and aggressive tumor biology and can subdivide patients into distinct risk groups. The primary endpoint is OS; secondary endpoints include disease-free survival (DFS), metastasis-free survival (MFS), and response to adjuvant therapy. The pZVR biomarker is incorporated as a surrogate predictor of metastatic propensity within the modeling framework. HDL-C and PTGDS are not posited as core drivers of the interaction but may be explored as ancillary systemic factors. Testing plan involves multi-omic integration and prospective validation to determine whether TIL-high/MSH6 status defines a niche where alternative or intensified adjuvant strategies are warranted.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
