# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In breast tumors with high Tumor-Infiltrating Lymphocyte (TIL) density, CXCR4 signaling drives upregulation of GLS (glutaminase) in tumor and/or stroma-associated cells, promoting a glutamine–dependent metabolic state that impairs local T-cell effector function. This metabolic crosstalk creates a microenvironment where T cells are functionally suppressed despite substantial TIL presence, leading to reduced therapy_response to checkpoint inhibitors. Consequently, tumors with the TIL-high and GLS-high phenotype will exhibit worse immunotherapy response than TIL-high/GLS-low tumors. The calculation is that GLS activity (and CXCR4-driven GLS upregulation) can be modulated by secondary modifiers—CCND1 (Cyclin D1), CTNNB1 (β-catenin), and the EMT_signature—which tune CXCR4 expression, glutamine metabolism, and T-cell localization, thereby altering immunotherapy outcomes. This yields a testable model: (i) TIL-high/GLS-high predicts poor therapy_response; (ii) GLS inhibition or CXCR4 blockade will restore T-cell function and improve therapy_response in this subset; (iii) secondary modifiers modulate the strength of this axis and can refine patient stratification for combination therapies.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
