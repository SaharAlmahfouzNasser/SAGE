# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Tier 1 synthesis highlights two robust but discordant findings — (A) noninvasive tumor-derived signals (urine methylation, EV mRNA/MDK) and immune infiltration are candidate biomarkers in NMIBC, and (B) metabolic marker DPYD is associated with poor prognosis. The key puzzle is: why do some TIL-rich NMIBC lesions still progress despite apparent immune infiltration?

## Working Hypothesis

In non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC), high infiltration of tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TIL) is not invariably associated with durable local control. We hypothesize that concurrent tumor-secreted extracellular vesicles (EVs) enriched for midkine (MDK) and/or high tumor DPYD (dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase) activity metabolically and immunosuppressively reprogram the tumor microenvironment (TME). Specifically, high MDK-containing EVs and/or elevated DPYD activity will blunt effector T cell function and promote an immunometabolic milieu that supports tumor progression, even among TIL-rich NMIBC lesions. Consequently, NMIBC tumors with high TIL density but concomitant high MDK EV burden and/or high DPYD expression/activity will show shorter progression-free intervals (PFI) compared with TIL-rich tumors with low MDK EVs and low DPYD, and TIL density alone will have reduced prognostic value unless integrated with MDK and DPYD. The expanded, testable predictions are: 
  (1) In NMIBC cohorts, TIL-high tumors with high MDK EV burden and/or high DPYD activity will have poorer PFI than TIL-high tumors with low MDK/DPYD, whereas TIL-low tumors with low MDK/DPYD will have the best PFI. 
  (2) MDK-containing EVs derived from NMIBC cells suppress CD8+ T cell proliferation and cytotoxic function in vitro, an effect that is amplified in the presence of high DPYD activity; pharmacologic or genetic inhibition of MDK or DPYD will restore T cell activity. 
  (3) In vivo, blocking MDK signaling or reducing DPYD activity will preserve or restore anti-tumor T cell function and delay progression in NMIBC models with TIL infiltration. 
  (4) A composite biomarker integrating TIL density, EV-MDK burden, and DPYD activity will outperform TIL density alone for predicting PFI and guiding surveillance/therapy decisions.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
