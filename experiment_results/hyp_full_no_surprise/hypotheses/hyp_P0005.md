# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

From the Ontologist synthesis, TLS/stromal engagement is common in MIBC but does not uniformly translate into better outcomes — why do some immune-infiltrated tumors still have poor survival? 2) Tier 1 paths used: I built on (a) the immunotherapy/immune-contexture insight (cross‑cancer biomarker rationale), (b) MSH6 as a DNA‑repair/prognostic anchor, and (c) the SARIFA tumor–adipocyte/stromal program that can suppress immune function. These three together define a puzzle of immune potential (TLS) versus molecular (MMR) and stromal brakes. 3) Tier 2 support: MDK/EV biomarkers, methylation markers (SDC2/TFPI2), metabolic/urinary signals (GLU, creatinine, microalbuminuria), and pZVR provide orthogonal evidence that (i) tumor cell programs and extracellular signals are measurable noninvasively and (ii) metabolic/stromal programs can correlate with aggressive, immune‑excluded phenotypes. These strengthen the plausibility that a stromal program can modulate an otherwise immune‑active tumor. 4) Why OS as endpoint: Overall survival is the most robust, clinically meaningful endpoint available across cohorts and best captures the net effect of interacting tumor, immune and stromal programs in MIBC. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I did not use the cross‑cancer 'HPV status' as a primary anchor (it informed rationale only) because it is a generalizer rather than an MIBC‑specific mechanistic node; I also deferred numerous Tier‑3/metabolic ideas (HDL‑C, exploratory glycan markers) to secondary_modifiers to keep the hypothesis tightly focused on one imaging feature plus two core anchors?

## Working Hypothesis

TLS-rich tumors in MIBC gain a survival advantage only when MSH6 function is intact, enabling TLS-mediated antigen presentation to stimulate anti-tumor immunity. This advantage is abrogated by SARIFA-high stromal signaling that suppresses TLS activity and antigen presentation, so TLS-rich tumors lose survival benefit in the presence of SARIFA-high or MSH6 deficiency.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
