# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In breast cancer, the prognostic benefit of high CD8+ TIL infiltration on overall survival is conditional on tumor cell apoptotic competence and metabolic/survival signaling. Specifically, tumors with high BCL2L11 (Bim) expression and low MTOR pathway activity will exhibit the strongest association between TIL density and improved OS, whereas tumors with low BCL2L11 or high MTOR activity will show attenuated or absent TIL-derived survival benefits. This interaction is further modulated by inflammatory state markers (e.g., S100A9) and immune-state indicators (e.g., CD44) as well as TGF-β–biased CD8 dynamics, which can dampen apoptotic execution and/or sustain tumor metabolism under immune pressure. The hypothesis yields testable predictions: (1) in TCGA-BRCA and similar cohorts, the TIL-OS association will be strongest in BCL2L11-high/MTOR-low tumors and weakest in BCL2L11-low/MTOR-high tumors; (2) the three-way interaction TIL × BCL2L11 × MTOR will significantly improve model fit for OS beyond any two-way interactions; (3) functional studies will show that MTOR inhibition or forced Bim upregulation sensitizes MTOR-high tumor cells to CTL-mediated killing; (4) modulation of S100A9 and CD44/TGF-β–related states will shift these interactions by altering CTL function or tumor cell susceptibility to apoptosis. Clinically, combining MTOR pathway modulation or Bim-enhancing strategies with adoptive/antigen-specific immunotherapies could convert TIL-rich but MTOR-driven tumors into OS responders.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.

## Caveats & Risks

- Gene expression does not equal protein activity; phospho-protein or functional assays are needed for confirmation.

- S100A9: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
