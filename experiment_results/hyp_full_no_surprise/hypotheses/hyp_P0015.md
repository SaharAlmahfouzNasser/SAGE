# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

Tumors with sparse or immature TLS, or TLS located distal from the tumor front, show diminished or absent TLS-driven local immune activation and thus reduced therapy_response.
