# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

In urothelial carcinoma (bladder cancer), deficiency of mismatch repair leading to MSI-H status increases tumor mutational burden and neoantigen load, driving an inflamed tumor immune microenvironment characterized by CD8+ T cell infiltration and interferon-gamma signaling, with upregulation of PD-L1 and intact antigen presentation pathways. This immunogenic milieu predicts superior clinical response to PD-1/PD-L1 blockade compared with microsatellite-stable tumors. Testable predictions: (i) MSI-H bladder tumors have higher TMB, higher neoantigen load, and greater MHC class I expression; (ii) they exhibit higher CD8+ T cell density, greater TCR clonality, elevated IFN-gamma–related gene signatures, and increased PD-L1; (iii) patients with MSI-H tumors show higher objective response rates and longer progression-free survival on PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors; (iv) in experimental systems, inducing MMR deficiency in bladder cancer lines increases mutational burden and enhances T cell recognition, while restoring MMR function reduces immunogenicity; (v) combination therapies that enhance antigen presentation or T cell infiltration may synergize with PD-1 blockade in MSI-H tumors. Experimental approach: (A) retrospective analyses of bladder cancer cohorts (TCGA, cBioPortal, clinical trial datasets) to quantify associations among MSI/MMR status, TMB/neoantigen load, immune signatures (CD8A, granzyme B, IFN-gamma score), PD-L1, and immunotherapy outcomes; (B) mechanistic validation using (1) isogenic bladder cancer cell lines with MMR gene knockouts and matched controls in T cell co-culture assays evaluating antigen presentation and T cell activation, (2) bladder cancer organoid–immune co-cultures, (3) in vivo syngeneic bladder cancer models to test PD-1 blockade response; (C) explore refinements with additional biomarkers (TMB thresholds, TCR clonality) and potential combination strategies.
