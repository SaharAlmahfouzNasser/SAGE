# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

The Ontologist highlights that noninvasive immune readouts (Immunoscore/TILs) and tumor-derived signals (urine DNA methylation, ADNP mRNA in CTCs/ctRNA) each predict NMIBC outcomes, yet immune-rich tumors do not always benefit from immune-based therapies or intravesical BCG. Why does local immune infiltration sometimes fail to translate into therapeutic response?

## Working Hypothesis

In NMIBC, the predictive value of Tumor-Infiltrating Lymphocyte (TIL) density for therapy_response is contingent on tumor-intrinsic ADNP expression. Specifically, high TIL density predicts favorable therapy_response to intravesical immunotherapy or systemic checkpoint blockade only when tumor ADNP expression is low; high ADNP expression drives chromatin remodeling that dampens antigen presentation or T cell effector function, offsetting the benefit of TILs. Therefore, a combined biomarker model using TIL density and ADNP status (with CD8A as a CD8+ T cell readout) will outperform TIL density alone in predicting therapy_response (endpoint). Secondary modifiers—urine DNA methylation signatures, CTC/ctRNA ADNP, HDL-C, HRR, and MSI—will refine this interaction and improve risk stratification by capturing tumor-epigenetic state and systemic immune context, but the primary mechanistic claim remains the TIL × ADNP interaction driving response or resistance.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.

## Caveats & Risks

- Gene expression does not equal protein activity; phospho-protein or functional assays are needed for confirmation.

## Alternative Directions

- urine DNA methylation signatures: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
- CTCs / ctRNA: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
