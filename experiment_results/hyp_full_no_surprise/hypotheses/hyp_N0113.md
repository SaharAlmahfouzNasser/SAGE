# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Tier 1 synthesis highlights that tumors with visible TLS-like lymphoid aggregates do not always show improved outcomes — why do some TLS-rich tumors fail to deliver a survival benefit? The Ontologist's core paths tie a membrane transporter (SLC6A4) into a miRNA-regulated Wnt/β‑catenin axis and show concurrent metabolic (glycolysis/HIF1A) and developmental (Hedgehog) programs that can reshape immune organization?

## Working Hypothesis

Do TLS-like lymphoid aggregates fail to confer a survival advantage in tumors with high tumor-intrinsic SLC6A4 activity driving Wnt/β-catenin (CTNNB1) signaling, thereby altering TLS composition and function and suppressing anti-tumor immunity? If this mechanism operates, TLS-rich tumors with high SLC6A4/CTNNB1 activity will have worse overall survival than TLS-rich tumors with low activity, indicating a context-dependent decoupling of TLS presence from clinical benefit.

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** observational analyses in TCGA/multi-omics cohorts to correlate TLS presence/quality with SLC6A4/CTNNB1 activity signatures and OS, controlling for confounders;

**Prediction 2:** spatial transcriptomics and multiplex imaging to compare TLS microenvironments in TLS-rich tumors stratified by pathway activity;

**Prediction 3:** in vitro studies manipulating SLC6A4 and CTNNB1 in tumor cells to assess β-catenin activity, chemokine/antigen-presentation gene expression, and lymphocyte recruitment in co-culture or organoid systems;


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
