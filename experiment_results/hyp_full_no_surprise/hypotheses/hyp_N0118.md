# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Intratumoral TLS-like lymphoid aggregates are expected to enhance local antitumor immunity, yet some TLS-rich tumors fail to respond to immunotherapy (here represented by BCG); the provided path links oncogenic drivers in stem-like tumor cells to PD-L1 upregulation, suggesting a tumor-intrinsic mechanism that could disable TLS function. 2) Tier 1 path(s) used: the single path connecting TMPRSS2/ERG expression in stem-like (PSMA+) cancer cells to PI3K-driven upregulation of PD-L1 (CD274). I built on TMPRSS2/ERG (oncogenic driver in the path) and CD274 (PD-L1, the immune checkpoint effector) because they form the most direct mechanistic axis that could neutralize TLS activity. 3) Tier 2 supporting evidence: PSMA expression on cancer stem cells (links stemness/therapy resistance to the axis) and PI3K pathway activation (mechanistic mediator of CD274 induction) reinforce the core TMPRSS2/ERG→PD-L1 hypothesis and suggest a cell-population (PSMA+ stem cells) and signaling mechanism (PI3K) for intervention. 4) Why therapy_response as the endpoint: the path explicitly connects PD-L1 modulation to BCG (an immunotherapy) and the clinical question is whether TLS-rich tumors still benefit from intravesical immunotherapy; 'therapy_response' is the most direct, clinically actionable endpoint. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded broader carcinoma-level edges and general organ-level context (bladder/urothelial descriptors) to keep the hypothesis mechanistically focused; PSMA and PI3K are moved to secondary_modifiers rather than being placed in the core hypothesis to respect the 1–2 anchor-gene limit?

## Working Hypothesis

In tumors with abundant TLS-like lymphoid aggregates, TMPRSS2-ERG expression in stem-like cancer cells drives upregulation of CD274 (PD-L1) via PI3K pathway signaling, functionally suppressing TLS-mediated antitumor immunity. Consequently, TLS-rich tumors with high TMPRSS2-ERG–CD274 activity will show reduced response to BCG therapy, identifying an immune-resistant TLS-rich subset.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
