# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

What molecular mechanisms determine whether immune structures translate into clinical benefit?

## Working Hypothesis

Primary hypothesis: In bladder cancer, a high stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) creates an extracellular-matrix–rich niche in which tumor cells expressing elevated GREM1 and MDK cooperate to remodel the ECM and invade lymphovascular spaces, increasing tumor cell dissemination and resulting in shorter progression-free intervals. Consequently, bladder tumors with high TSR and co-overexpression of GREM1 and MDK will have significantly shorter PFI compared with TSR-low or GREM1/MDK-low tumors, defining a stroma-driven, metastasis-prone subgroup that may benefit from anti-stromal or anti–MDK/GREM1 therapies. The hypothesis adheres to the constraint of a single imaging feature (TSR) and 1–2 anchor genes (GREM1, MDK).


| Element | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Imaging feature | Stromal-to-tumor ratio (TSR) |
| Anchor gene(s) | GREM1, MDK |
| Primary endpoint | PFI |

# 3. Clinically Testable Predictions

**Prediction 1:** In cohorts with TSR and GREM1/MDK data, the interaction of TSR with GREM1/MDK expression will predict PFI beyond either factor alone.

**Prediction 2:** TSR-high tumors with elevated GREM1/MDK will show biomarkers of invasion/ECM remodeling and higher incidence of nodal or distant progression.

**Prediction 3:** In retrospective cohort data, the prognostic benefit of the imaging feature will be modified by the anchor gene expression level (significant interaction term).


# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.


# 5. Strengths, Caveats & Alternative Directions

## Strengths

- Interaction-first biomarker strategy — asks WHY the imaging feature sometimes fails, not just whether it predicts outcome.

## Caveats & Risks

- Hypothesis is computationally derived and requires experimental validation.

## Alternative Directions

- CTCs: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
- EV-mRNA signatures: Could serve as an alternative mechanistic axis if the primary hypothesis is not supported.
