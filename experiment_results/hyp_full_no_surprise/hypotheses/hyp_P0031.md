# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

MSH6 loss should increase neoantigen load and recruit TILs, which ought to slow progression, yet many MSH6-deficient bladder cancers still progress or recur. ## Working Hypothesis

MSH6-deficient bladder tumors generate high neoantigen loads that recruit tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs). However, durable tumor control requires robust systemic immune/nutritional competence as captured by the Prognostic Nutritional Index (PNI). Specifically, among MSH6-deficient, TIL-high bladder cancers, individuals with low PNI exhibit impaired TIL cytotoxic function (reduced granzyme B and perforin, diminished IFN-γ signaling, and elevated exhaustion markers such as PD-1/LAG-3), leading to immune escape and shorter progression-free interval (PFI). In contrast, patients with preserved or high PNI maintain functional TIL activity and experience longer PFI. Tumor-derived modulators (e.g., MDK) and extracellular vesicle (EV) cargo, along with host urinary/metabolic signals, act as secondary modifiers that can further suppress or sustain TIL function, accounting for cases where high TIL density does not predict favorable outcomes. The core testable predictions are: (a) a significant interaction between TIL density (or CD8+ TIL metrics) and PNI on PFI in MSH6-deficient tumors; (b) TIL functional state correlates with PNI independent of TIL density; (c) MDK/EV/urinary metabolite signatures add predictive value beyond TIL density and PNI; and (d) interventions that improve nutritional/immunometabolic status or inhibit tumor-derived suppressive signals can shift at-risk patients toward longer PFI.

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
