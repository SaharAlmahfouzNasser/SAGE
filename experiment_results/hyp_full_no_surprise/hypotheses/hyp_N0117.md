# 1. Clinical Question & Core Hypothesis

## Clinical Question

Organized intratumoral immune structures (TLS) or heavy lymphoid infiltration should limit progression, yet some immune-infiltrated tumors still progress—why? 2) Tier 1 paths used: (a) PSMA is expressed in cancer stem–like cells (path 3), providing a candidate tumor-intrinsic, therapy-resistant cell population; (b) PI3K signaling is functionally linked to PD-L1–mediated programs (path 6), suggesting a PD-L1↔PI3K axis that could enable immune escape. I built the core hypothesis on the intersection of stemness (PSMA+) and immune-checkpoint signaling (PD-L1). 3) Tier 2 supporting evidence: TMPRSS2/ERG cooperativity with PI3K (path 5) supports that oncogenic programs can amplify PI3K-driven immune-evasive signaling; BCG/PD-L1 links (paths 7,10,15) illustrate that PD-L1 is a dynamic, therapy-modulated node, reinforcing PD-L1's role in shaping immune outcomes. 4) Why PFI chosen as endpoint: progression-free interval is most sensitive to early relapse/progression driven by therapy-resistant stem-like populations and thus directly tests whether PSMA+/PD-L1–mediated mechanisms allow tumor progression despite organized immune infiltrates. OS is more confounded by later-line treatments; therapy-response proxies in TCGA-BRCA are limited. 5) Deliberate exclusions: I excluded TMPRSS2/ERG and PIK3CA from the core anchors to keep the mechanistic claim compact (max 2 anchors); androgen-deprivation and BCG are contextually relevant but not central to breast cohorts in TCGA-BRCA, so they are placed outside the core hypothesis to avoid overfitting the claim to cross-tissue inferences?

## Working Hypothesis

In TLS-rich breast tumors, a subpopulation of PSMA (FOLH1)-expressing cancer stem–like cells activates a PD-L1–dependent PI3K-AKT signaling axis, promoting immune evasion and continued tumor progression despite organized TLS. Disrupting PSMA, PD-L1, or PI3K signaling will reduce this immune-evading program and extend progression-free interval (PFI).

# 4. Minimum Viable Validation Plan

## A. Retrospective Cohort Analysis

**Data source:** TCGA-BLCA (Firehose Legacy), ~400 patients
- Cox PH model with interaction term for the imaging feature and anchor gene
- Kaplan-Meier 2x2 stratification

## B. Tissue-Level Validation

**Methods:** Multiplex IF / IHC / spatial transcriptomics

**Key readouts:**
- Immune structure markers (e.g., CD20, CD3, PNAd)
- Effector cell localization (CD8) relative to tumor nests
- Anchor gene protein / phospho-protein expression
- Stromal features (collagen density by SHG, if applicable)

**Goal:** Confirm that the proposed mechanism operates at the spatial / tissue level.

## C. Functional Validation

**Models:** Ex vivo organotypic cultures, 3D co-culture, syngeneic mouse models

**Perturbations:** Genetic (KO/rescue) or pharmacologic modulation of anchor gene

**Readouts:** T-cell infiltration, cytotoxicity (granzyme B / IFN-gamma), tumor growth / survival

**Goal:** Establish causal relationship between anchor gene activity and immune efficacy.
